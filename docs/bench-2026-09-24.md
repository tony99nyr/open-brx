# Bench runbook: the 2026-09-24 sitting

One sitting in priority order: P0 BLE reliability first, then the screamers transport steps, then one real match,
then the outdoor range ladder if there is time and space. Each step gives the command, the pass or fail rule, and
what to log. The method is the [`bench-session` skill](../.claude/skills/bench-session/SKILL.md): the agent drives
the tools, Tony handles the hardware and types `1` when a step is done, and every result has a control. Stop a
block when time runs out; the order is the priority.

| block | what | rows | time | kit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | preflight | - | 10 min | everything |
| 1 | connect reliability | F297, F293 | 45 min | 1 gun and headset, laptop, both Pixels |
| 2 | screamers transport | F269, F270, F272 | 45 min | 1 gun and headset, laptop |
| 3 | trigger release order | F308 | 10 min | 1 gun, laptop |
| 4 | one real match | S56, F308, F291, F292, F298, F296, F309 | 60 min | 2 guns, both Pixels, MC |
| 5 | outdoor range ladder | F275 | 60 min | 2 guns, outdoor, the rig |

Blocks 1 to 4 fit one indoor sitting of about three hours. Block 5 needs outdoor space; run it on another day if
there is none.

## Conventions

- Shell variables for every command below:
  - `PY=/mnt/c/Users/Tony/.brx-mcp/venv/Scripts/python.exe`: Windows Python, the only one with Bluetooth
    (`wsl-dev-runbook.md`).
  - `WPY=/home/tony/gitrepos/battlecompany/.venv/bin/python`: WSL Python, for frame generation only.
  - `GUN=<address>`: the gun's BLE address from `$PY -m brx_mcp scan`.
- Run every command from the repo root. Arguments go on the command line only: environment variables do not cross
  from WSL to Windows.
- **Single-quote every frame** in a `raw-bytes` argument. In double quotes the shell expands `$AMMO` to nothing.
- Bench audio is `$VOL,65`. Never end a block on a bare `$CLEAR` (F11). Power-cycle the gun at the end of a block
  and disconnect the MCP session.
- A **LOCK-UP**, a **LINK DROP** and a **BAD FRAME** are defined in the screamers sheet ("Rules for every session").
  On a lock-up: send `$PLAYX,0,*`, then `$HLED,,6,*`, then `$STOP,*`, note which got through, then power-cycle the
  gun and headset and re-arm before the next step.
- Phone state and logs are read from WSL with `$PY mcp/tools/webview_eval.py "<js>" --serial <phone ip:port>`.
  Phone log lines carry `[HH:MM:SS]`, so a phone time is good to about 1 s.

## Block 0: preflight (10 min)

1. The preflight in [`gotchas.md`](gotchas.md) ("Before a bench session"): kill stale `brx_mcp` processes, then
   power-cycle the gun and the headset.
2. Both Pixels: re-enable wireless debugging and `adb connect` each one. Confirm the installed app version on each:
   `$PY mcp/tools/webview_eval.py "window.brx.APP_VER" --serial <phone>`. Log both versions.
3. Check what is on `main` now, because blocks 4.6 and 4.8 depend on it:
   - `git log origin/main --oneline -i --grep=shotgun | head -3`: has the Shotgun's 700 ms cadence landed (F291/F308)?
   - `git log origin/main --oneline --grep=F309 | head -3`: has brx3's F309 transport change landed?
   If a phone needs the newer build, cut and install it now (`cd app && npm run android:install`).

## Block 1: connect reliability, P0 (45 min)

The question: how long does a cold connect take, how often does the first attempt work, and does the headset drop
in the first 30 s? The laptop run is the control: it has no Android Bluetooth stack in the way.

**1.1 Laptop, 10 cold connects (F297).**
`$PY -m brx_mcp connect-metrics $GUN --runs 10`
Before each run the tool asks Tony to power-cycle the gun and the headset, then press Enter.
- **Log:** the summary: first-attempt success rate, link time median, p90 and max, headset drops, never-linked
  count, errors. Keep the JSONL path it prints.
- **Pass or fail:** no bar yet; this is the first measurement. Flag any run with a headset or BLE drop inside 30 s.

**1.2 Each Pixel, 5 cold connects (F297).** For each try: power-cycle the gun and the headset, wait for the headset
to settle, then Tony taps the gun in the picker and starts a stopwatch; he stops it at "linked" on the HUD.
Then read the phone's side:
`$PY mcp/tools/webview_eval.py "window.brx.log.slice(-40)" --serial <phone>`
- **Log per try:** the stopwatch time; the log's "connecting to", "connect i/5 failed after N ms" and "linked"
  lines; whether "*** gun disconnected ***" or "gun keeps dropping" appears within 30 s of the link.
- **Pass or fail:** no bar yet. Compare against 1.1: a phone that drops while the laptop did not points F293 at
  the phone.

**1.3 Only if 1.2 shows the link loop (F293).** One variable at a time, A/B/A:
1. On the looping phone, turn on Developer options, then "Bluetooth HCI snoop log", then toggle Bluetooth off and
   on. Reproduce the loop for about 60 s, then `adb -s <phone> bugreport bugreport-f293-on.zip`. Keep the phone
   still for 5 to 10 min while it writes.
2. **B:** turn off Fast Pair and Nearby scanning (Settings, Google, Devices and sharing, Devices, "Scan for nearby
   devices" off). Power-cycle the headset, connect again, and watch 60 s.
3. **A again:** turn scanning back on and repeat step 2's connect.
- **Log:** loop yes or no in A, B and A; each bugreport file name; the time of each drop from the phone log.
- **Pass or fail:** the question is answered only if B differs from both A runs. The bugreport is read at the desk
  (`python -m brx_mcp.btsnoop` decodes the ATT traffic inside it).

## Block 2: screamers transport (45 min)

One gun, one laptop, `raw-bytes`. First arm the gun with the bench victim head from
[`bench-perks-2026-09-18.md`](bench-perks-2026-09-18.md) ("Roles and arming"), then run each step's control before
its variant. The step definitions are screamers A4, A7, A7b, A7c and A8; the command-line table there is the same
as below. Every run ends with a `$PING,*` liveness check unless the plan ends mid-frame.

Generate the two A8 frames once (magazine 32 and magazine 30; 111 B each):

```
WEAP32=$($WPY mcp/tools/armgen.py 1 5 ar 2>/dev/null | grep '^\$WEAP,0,')
WEAP30=$($WPY mcp/tools/armgen.py 1 5 ar t16=30 t39=30 2>/dev/null | grep '^\$WEAP,0,')
A7='$PLAY,U37,3,10,,,,,*$PLAY,U37,3,10,,,,,*$PLAY,U37,3,10,,,,,*$PLAY,U37,3,10,,,,,*$PLAY,U37,3,10,,,,,*$PLAY,U37,3,10,,,,,*$PLAY,U37,3,10,,,,,*$PLAY,U37,3,10,,,,,*$PLAY,U37,3,10,,,,,*$QUERY,*'
```

**2.1 A7c, the receive-ring boundary.** Alternate three times:
- `$PY -m brx_mcp raw-bytes $GUN --stream '$PING,*' --repeat 146 --read-ms 5000`
- `$PY -m brx_mcp raw-bytes $GUN --stream '$PING,*' --repeat 147 --read-ms 5000`

Then once each with `--phone-pacing` added (the control).
- **Log:** `PONG=` per run, and the liveness line.
- **Pass or fail:** 146 at zero gap and at phone pacing answer 146 each. The number that matters is how many of
  147 answer at zero gap.

**2.2 A7, short-frame burst.** Three runs each:
- zero gap: `$PY -m brx_mcp raw-bytes $GUN --stream "$A7" --repeat 10 --read-ms 5000`
- block pause: `$PY -m brx_mcp raw-bytes $GUN --stream "$A7" --repeat 10 --phone-pacing --block-frames 10 --block-pause-ms 300 --read-ms 5000`
- **Log:** `QUERY=` per run (10 expected; each missing reply is a lost frame), and any LOCK-UP.
- **Pass or fail:** a pacing passes when all three runs return 10. If zero gap loses frames and the block pause
  does not, that is the F269 number: turn on `WRITE_PACING.blockFrames = 10`, `blockPauseMs = 300`.

**2.3 A8, long-frame burst.** Three runs each:
- zero gap: `$PY -m brx_mcp raw-bytes $GUN --stream "$WEAP32$WEAP30" --repeat 25 --read-ms 10000`
- phone pacing: the same, plus `--phone-pacing`
- with response (F270): the phone-paced line, plus `--with-response`

Inside each 10 s read window Tony fires one round.
- **Log:** the printed last `$ALCD` (the magazine), per run.
- **Pass or fail:** the last frame sets magazine 30, so after one shot `$ALCD` must read 29. Anything else is a
  BAD FRAME for that run. ⚠️ One read per run proves only the LAST frame applied; a lost middle frame is visible
  only if it corrupts the next one. Count failed runs, not lost frames.
- **Decision it feeds:** zero gap fails and phone pacing passes means pacing works as is. Phone pacing fails and
  `--with-response` passes turns F270 on.

**2.4 A4, the stale-token parser reset.** Three runs of each, alternating:
- no reset: `$PY -m brx_mcp raw-bytes $GUN --segment '$AMMO,0,10,50,1,*' --segment '$AMMO,0,17,50,1' --segment '$AMMO,0,23,50,1,*' --allow-incomplete --read-ms 10000`
- reset: the same with `--segment '$*'` before the last segment.

Tony fires one round in the read window.
- **Log:** the last `$ALCD` magazine per run.
- **Pass or fail:** with the reset, 22 every time (23 minus the shot). Without it, the prediction is a BAD FRAME
  (not 22). If both read 22, the reset is not needed.

**2.5 A7b, a split frame across a gap.** Three runs at each gap:
- 60 ms: `$PY -m brx_mcp raw-bytes $GUN --segment '$AMMO,0,10,50,1,*' --segment '$AMMO,0,2' --segment '3,50,1,*' --delays-ms 0,60 --read-ms 10000`
- 2 s: the same with `--delays-ms 0,2000`

Tony fires one round in the read window.
- **Pass or fail:** 22 in all six runs: the parser keeps a split frame across a gap.

End the block with a power-cycle of the gun and the headset.

## Block 3: trigger release order, F308 part 1 (10 min)

Does every trigger release (`$BUT,0,0`) reach the host, in order with the `$ALCD` stream, under full auto? The
recoil burst reset depends on it.

1. The agent connects with the MCP tool, then arms the stock AR: `$WPY mcp/tools/armgen.py 1 5 ar`, sent with
   `send_batch`.
2. Tony fires five bursts with the trigger held about 2 s each, and releases fully between them. Type `1`.
3. The agent reads `get_events` in one snapshot.
- **Log:** the count of `$BUT,0,1` and `$BUT,0,0`, and for each burst the seq of its last `$ALCD` against its
  `$BUT,0,0`.
- **Pass or fail:** 5 presses and 5 releases, and each release comes after its burst's last `$ALCD`. Control: one
  single shot before the bursts must show one press and one release.

## Block 4: one real match (60 min)

Two guns, both Pixels, Mission Control. Start MC from `mcp/`:
`setsid nohup ../.venv/bin/python -m brx_mcp.mc --advertise 192.168.0.55 --bench-volume`. Restart MC between
matches, never while a match is ARMED or LIVE. Read each phone with `webview_eval.py`.

**4.1 The new respawn rules (0.4.5 field check, step 4).** A timed respawn: no protection by default, weapon live
0.5 s after the countdown. A station respawn: 2 s visible protection, trigger live at once, white shield blink.
- **Pass or fail:** each behaviour as described, seen on both phones.

**4.2 S56, the hit relay.** Play a 5-minute TDM. At the end read both phones:
`$PY mcp/tools/webview_eval.py "JSON.stringify(window.brx.engine.state().lastLife)" --serial <phone>` (and `.life`).
- **Log:** for each shooter, DEALT and the PARTIAL flag; for each victim, TOOK and its sources.
- **Pass or fail:** the relay is best-effort, so the check is a count: a shooter's DEALT should equal the sum of
  what the victims TOOK from that shooter. Log every mismatch and whether PARTIAL was set.

**4.3 F308 part 2, the Assault Rifle ladder.** The numbers are the `assault_rifle` row of the recoil table in
[`spec/node.md`](spec/node.md) §3.15. On 2026-09-23 it read degraded 70 from round 6, heavy 40 from round 8, and
crisp again after 600 ms of quiet; brx1's balance lane is still moving it, so read the row again on the day. Give
one player the AR. While Tony fires, the agent reads `window.brx.engine.state().aim`:
- bursts of 5 with a pause between: `aim` stays empty (crisp);
- a held trigger: `reason: 'recoil'` at the degraded accuracy from the `after_shots` round, then the heavy accuracy
  from the `after_heavy` round;
- `settle_ms` of quiet: back to crisp.
- **Log:** the round and the accuracy at each change, and Tony's word on the feel.
- **Pass or fail:** the three transitions at the table's rounds and values.
- **Also for F291:** the Charge Rifle's tap now does 16, not 20, through `$WEAP` t37 (`3759cd68`); the charge
  stays 70. One charged shot then taps on a victim: read the victim's pools and log the drop per tap. Pass: 70
  for the charge, 16 for each tap.

**4.4 F292, the Toxin Rifle.** One player on the Toxin Rifle; hit the other once.
- **Listen:** H23 on the poison hit, then V4G "Cough!" on each tick. Control: an AR hit plays neither.
- **Wire:** read the victim's pools between ticks (`window.brx.engine.state()`). Each tick must move exactly one pool
  by the tick amount (4).
- **Lethal tick:** let a tick kill a low player. MC's recap must book a credited `death` with `dot: true`.
- **Pass or fail:** all three.

**4.5 F298, the Shields preset.** One match on Shields (45/0/105). Watch the recharge cues and the pool readout on
both HUDs.

**4.6 The Shotgun, only if its 700 ms cadence is on `main` (block 0, step 3).** Fire it at 5 m and at 10 m.
- **Log:** the time between shots from the phone log, and hits landed out of 10 at each distance.
- **Pass or fail:** about 700 ms between shots, and 5 m hits land. The 10 m count is a data point for F275.

**4.7 F296 and full screen (0.4.5 field check, steps 6 and 7).** Does the slowed `$HLOOP,1,2500` rearm read as
"down" and not as a hit? Does the Android status bar stay hidden during play on both phones?

**4.8 F309, only if brx3's change is on `main`.** brx3's four checks:
1. A Pixel with Wi-Fi and mobile data both on: what does `transport` report, `wifi` or `cellular`?
2. Both phones on cellular through the tunnel: the chip reads "Internet: 2 of 2 phones · 2 on cellular", in green.
3. One phone on the field Wi-Fi through the tunnel: the chip drops to zones, in grey.
4. Mobile data off mid-lobby: the count falls within one heartbeat (about 2 s).

## Block 5: the headset word's range, F275 (60 min, outdoor)

The procedure is the F275 row: an F231-style shaded ladder against `t13`, with the same rig, the same shading fix
and the discard-the-first-two-pulls control (F232). Set a Shotgun's `t13` to a few values, fire at a victim at
5 m and at 25 m, and find where the headset word (20) stops arriving. `t13` is a carrier frequency, 140 Hz per step
(levers §20), so read the result as a frequency ladder.
- **Log:** per `t13` value and distance: words sent, headset words received.
- **Pass or fail:** a `t13` value where the word arrives at 5 m and not at 25 m. That value is the edge of the
  close-range balance band.
