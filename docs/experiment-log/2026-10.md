### 2026-10-02 (desk) · brx3 · F437: the go-live klaxon cut the character taunt

**Report:** bench 2026-10-02 (0.4.16, Tony): at the start of a game the character line began "no where to hide", but
only "no.." played.
**Cause:** `_spawn` sent the spawn line on the queue slot (`$PLAY,,4,6,<id>,,,,*`), then the klaxon
(`$PLAY,U16,4,6,,,,,*`) in a separate write 150 ms later. The klaxon is on the interrupt slot (token 1), and an id
there cuts whatever plays (`docs/manual/sound.md`).
**Change:** the klaxon and the line now go as one two-slot frame, `$PLAY,U16,4,6,<line>,,,,*`, after `$SFLASH`.
This is Callsign's game-end form (a sting plus "victory", both played). A first fix sent the klaxon first, then the
line. The line then waited about 0.4 s on the phone for the klaxon (F419), so the burst settled late, and three
F416 re-send tests failed. A pair that does not fit one frame still goes as the klaxon, then the line. The shield
fill stays last (X3). The X3/F437 test pins one frame, and no interrupt-slot `$PLAY` or `$PLAYX` follows it.
**Confidence:** desk only. Bench: `bench-standard-2026-09-29.md` step 10 (3 starts at volume 80).
