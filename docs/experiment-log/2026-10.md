# Experiment log: 2026-10

### 2026-10-02 (desk) · brx3 · F437: the go-live klaxon cut the character taunt

**Report:** bench 2026-10-02 (0.4.16, Tony): at the start of a game the character line began "no where to hide", but
only "no.." played.
**Cause:** `_spawn` sent the spawn line on the queue slot (`$PLAY,,4,6,<id>,,,,*`), then the klaxon
(`$PLAY,U16,4,6,,,,,*`) in a separate write 150 ms later. The klaxon is on the interrupt slot (token 1), and an id
there cuts whatever plays (`docs/manual/sound.md`).
**Change:** the klaxon now goes before the line, and the line queues behind it. The shield fill stays last (X3). The
X3 test pins the order, and no interrupt-slot `$PLAY` or `$PLAYX` follows the line. With the old order the test fails.
**Confidence:** desk only. Bench: `bench-standard-2026-09-29.md` step 10 (3 starts at volume 80).

### 2026-10-02 (desk) · brx3 · F438: a player's own shot no longer hurts them

**Report:** bench 2026-10-02, indoors, TDM with friendly fire off (`$GSET` token 1 = 0, from the same game's later
heads): green's sniper shot bounced off a wall into his own headset and killed him. The phone named his own id as the
killer; MC's feed had no killer (`scoring.py _kill_pair` already credits nobody when killer equals victim).
**Finding (unproven):** the firmware drops a self-hit only within 124 ms of its own shot, and FF-off gates by team.
A wall bounce arrives within milliseconds, yet neither stopped it. The step 11 A/B tests this.
**Change:** the phone treats a `$HIR` whose shooter id equals its own `player_num` as a self-hit. One `$LIFE` gives
back what the paired `$HP` took, measured from the pools the gun last reported. A lethal one revives at once through
the timed revive burst, with a negative `$LIFE` drain last, so a self-kill never heals. Poison and smoke are kept,
there is no spawn line, and nothing reaches MC. The echo window drops exactly one zero `$LCD` (the gun sends
`$HP,0,0,0` then `$LCD,0,0,0,1,1,1`). A self word within 1000 ms of another player's damaging word takes the normal
path. The stage mirrors all of it.
**Review:** three polish rounds. Round 2 caught the `$LCD` twin still booking the death (the field bug itself).
**Confidence:** desk only. The revive plus drain on a gun that just died is bench-unproven.

### 2026-10-02 (desk) · brx3 · F439: the death stop no longer kills the scream

**Report:** bench 2026-10-02 (0.4.16, step 11.8), three fast kills. Run 3 logged "low-health alert: hp 7 < 15", then
"write death: stop 1/1 for a clip ahead of the scream", and Tony heard neither Health critical nor the scream. The
same thing happened on Shields. A sibling: a heartbeat written in the death second played after the scream.
**Reading:** the native scream interrupts the playing clip at once (F375 agrees), so the stop cut the scream itself.
A body clip written just before the death reaches the gun after the scream starts and queues behind it.
**Change:** with a known native scream, no stop at death. The model puts the scream in front, and an interrupted
announcer line is requeued after it. Body cues queued behind the scream are stopped after it ends + 300 ms, never past
the first announcer line, and none is stopped with under 250 ms left. No heartbeat goes out while dead or before the
scream ends. The F149 stop stays only when no scream is known.
**Review:** three polish rounds (pool takes, short clips, late writes, lazy collection, stale docs).
**Confidence:** desk only; step 12 (six fast kills, every scream heard).
