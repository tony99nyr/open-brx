### 2026-10-16 (bench + desk) · brx1 benched, brx4 fixed, brx5 read the seam · F537: the player's hill flapped while its own scan was closed

Bench (brx1, C.34, 0.4.23, two phone hills at the default -75 dBm, the player between them): the player phone logged its
control station "left" at -60 to -67 dBm and "PRESENT" 1-2 s later, about a dozen times in four minutes, and adopted

  flood guard closes the PLAYER's scan for 5 s, past the 4 s expiry (the hill phone's own scan is exempt in a control
  game; the player's was not). The other 6 flaps line up with scan reopens ("re-assert after the scan reopened").
- Fix (brx4): BeaconWatch reports its scan's stop and start (`onClose`, `onOpen`), and app.js pauses the player's
  Presence across the gap. While paused, `tick` changes nothing; on resume every per-entry clock (seen, below the band,
  sighted, the dwell, the recent window) moves on by the paused span, clamped at 0 for a clock that stepped back. A
  hill's Presence never pauses, so the shared hill cases and the Stick are unchanged.
