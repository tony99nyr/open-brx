## 3. The issues

### 1. SMG kit — the `$WEAP` frame has no `,*` terminator  🔴

**Verified directly**: `WeaponCatalog().resolve(wid, 0, None, "outdoor")` over all 16 catalogue
weapons — 15 end in `,*`, the SMG does not. It is the only one.

```
$WEAP,0,2,40,0,0,7,0,,,,,,2,100,100,850,54,216,2500,0,0,100,100,,5,,,G03,,,,D26,D25,D24,D18,D11,,,,54,108,75,100
                                                                                              ^^^ no ,*
```

`*` dispatches a frame. `app/src/brxlink.js:29-41` (F341) already documents the consequence: a frame
that loses its `*` puts its text in front of the next frame's tokens. So the gun never loaded a slot-0
weapon, never emitted `$ALCD`, and `app/src/engine.js:2303` reported `no_echo` when the 1500 ms echo
window (`engine.js:298`) closed empty.

**The retry was self-sustaining.** `engine.js:2001` rewrites the whole head — starting `$CLEAR,*`,
~30 frames / ~180 paced BLE chunks — on every relink *while `headEcho` is null*. The one condition the
missing byte creates is the one that triggers an endless full re-push.

**Why every diagnostic pointed at the headset.** `engine.js:7405`:
`preflight: { gun_linked: this.bleUp, headset_ok: !!this.headEcho, ... }` — **`headset_ok` is the
head-echo flag**, nothing to do with the headset, which was attached throughout (`hds.59 (joined)` at
19:59:10 and 20:01:41 UTC). `brxlink.js:734-735` then turns the resulting flap into *"Is the headset
on?"* and *"Power-cycle the headset"*. That is why Tony recycled gun, headset and phone, and swapped
phones twice.

**Timeline** (`p0/ack-config-failures.log`): config `aa1b675d` refused **33×** 15:51:06 → 16:08:01
across three phones, `err: "no_echo"`; the other three guns acked it `ok:true` first try at
15:51:08-09; at 16:08:25 a kit without the SMG acked `ok:true` instantly.

**Introduced** `310fc42c` "Make SMG headex emission effective", 2026-09-22. Latent 4 weeks — today was
the first time the SMG shipped as a *primary*. Mechanism: the SMG's `capture.frame`
(`mcp/brx_mcp/mc/weapons.json:172-226`) is a 44-field single-emitter capture with no t42 cell, but the
row declares `overrides.t42 = 100` because `compile.py:1482-1489` requires it for an invented headset
word. `_T["headset_range_indoor"] = 42` and `put()` writes `p[43]` (`compile.py:1266,1406-1407`). The
guard at `compile.py:1524-1525` tests that cell for **emptiness** — on a 44-field frame `p[43]` is
`"*"`, not empty — so the write lands on the terminator. `_override_index`
(`compile.py:1575-1601`) has no bounds or terminator check.

**Two false greens, separate bug.** The two acks that "passed" echoed `$LCD,0,0,0,0,0,0,*`.
`protocol/brx-protocol.md:230`: a zeroed `$LCD` after `$SPAWN` means **no config is loaded**.
`engine.js:5535` and `:5549` accept any `LCD`/`ALCD` with no shape test, so MC marked the gun ready
twice while it provably had nothing loaded.

**Blast radius — the broken frame is committed as expected:** `mcp/tests/fixtures/compile_characterisation.json:30174`
pins it, and `webapp/mc/src/api/contract.gen.ts:406` (`WEAPON_MOCK_FIELDS`) ships it to the `?mock`
demo. **And the guard that would have caught it exists but is not wired in**: `protocol.validate_frame`
/ `_FRAME_RE` (`mcp/brx_mcp/protocol.py:60,272-279`) is called only from the lab instrument
(`server.py:121,148`), never in the MC compile or push path; `app/src` has no terminator check at all.
`brx-mcp send` would have refused this frame — which is why no bench caught it.

**❗ The dual-emitter path is exonerated.** Match 3 shipped a `t1=2` **Shotgun** in slot 1 to three
players including PL1's own gun — every frame terminated, all four acked first try. Issues 1 and 2
share the weapon family by coincidence, not by cause. **Do not merge them.**

**Fix:** (a) `compile.py:1524` must treat `*` as "no cell" and *extend* the frame, keeping the
terminator last; same for the overrides loop; add bounds/terminator checks to `_override_index`.
(b) Gate every compiled head through `protocol.validate_frame`. (c) Regenerate the two fixtures.
(d) Shape-test `$LCD` vs `$ALCD` in `engine.js:5535/5549`. (e) Stop `preflight.headset_ok` meaning
"head echoed" — rename or split.

**Bench first (5 min, one gun, no IR rig).** Arm normally (`$VOL,65`, `$CLEAR,*`, `$START,*`, a
`$PSET`), then write today's SMG frame **with `,*` appended**, then `$SPAWN,,*`:
`$ALCD,54,100,0,108,0,*` ⇒ the terminator was the whole bug. No `$ALCD` ⇒ the firmware may also reject
t42 on a 44-field capture; retry terminated with t42 dropped (43 fields + `,*`).
⚠️ Do **not** send the unterminated frame via `brx-mcp send` (it refuses); if you want that control use
`rawbytes.py` or the phone, and follow with `PARSER_RESET` (`$*`) or you carry a wedged parser into the
next step. End re-armed, never on a bare `$CLEAR`.

**Not a known followup.** No F-number for frame termination or `no_echo` on a kit. **New row needed.**

### 2. Secondary weapon — this is **F542**, re-rank to 🔴 P0

`docs/FOLLOWUPS.md:212`, filed 2026-10-16, *one day before this session*. Its tail records a `$QUERY`
fix **failing on hardware on 0.4.24** and a revert to the assumed-after-`swap_ms` rule. **The code that
played today is that reverted code, at field scale, for the first time.**

**cause 1 — structural (~60%): the gun has no readable "selected slot".**
`app/src/ammo.js:577` `altPressed()` toggles a pointer and **writes nothing to the gun** (the gun acts
on the physical button). `ammo.js:636` `switchTick()` then **unconditionally** commits the guessed slot
after `swap_ms`. The only correction is a real `$ALCD`, which the gun emits **only on an ammo event** —
so the phone re-converges on the next shot or magazine rise and nothing else. `$ALCD` token 3 *is* the
live slot, but the `$QUERY`→`$LCD` read built for this was proved wrong on 2026-10-16: **`$LCD` token 4
is the slot of the last SHOT, not the selected slot** (`docs/experiment-log/2026-10.md:955-967`).

The divergence window is exactly *"ALT, then reload before firing"* — what a player does when swapping
to a secondary they know is empty.

Evidence (`p0/slot-and-reload.log`):
- **184 `assumed` vs 6 `confirmed` swaps** — 97% never verified.
- **32 of 135 in-life consecutive ALT-press pairs targeted the same slot** (~24%), only possible if an
  `$ALCD` in between pulled the phone back — direct proof the gun did not move. An undercount.
- **26 of 26 reload failures follow an assumed slot change or a bare spawn. Zero follow a confirmed one.**
- **Match 1 had one `$WEAP` slot and ALT inert (`$BMAP,1,98`): 0 assumed swaps, 0 reload failures.**

Three *bench-proven* reasons the gun swallows a press: after `$SPAWN` it ignores a slot change until
the first trigger pull (`docs/experiment-log/2026-10.md:540`, 0/6 vs 4/4); it refuses ALT during a
reload (bench 2026-10-02); a lever inside a swap reloads the OLD slot while the swap completes.

❌ **Ruled out, do not spend bench time:** the `$BMAP,1,100,0,1,99,99` cycle list — with slots 0-3
loaded, "ALT only cycles two" (`docs/experiment-log/2026-09.md:434`); F233 closed as superseded.
`swap_ms` is correct (`compile.py:2714` takes `max(t15)`, matching each player's frames).

**cause 2 — bug (~15%): proof the gun did NOT swap is thrown away. One line, no bench.**
A round on the **source** slot inside the window proves no swap happened. `ammo.js:361`'s confirm
branch requires `slot !== switching.from` so it never sees it; `ammo.js:384/394` correctly pull the
pointer back — then **`switchTick` overrides both anyway** and logs `(no shot yet)` when a shot did
arrive. `cancelSwap()` (`ammo.js:566`) is never called from `onAmmo`. **Already a spec requirement**:
`docs/spec/node.md:737` *"A reply on the old slot closes the card without a swap."*
⚠️ That spec is now self-contradictory — `:737` describes the reverted `$QUERY` path, `:152` the live
assumed path. Fix both.

**cause 3 — independent (~20%): the shotgun needs the handle HELD.**
⚠️ **Revised — see Appendix E9.** Tony later reported that *holding made no difference* and that
there was *no reload sound*. Wire evidence then showed the gun sitting on **slot 0** while his HUD
was on the shotgun. So his shotgun complaint is **cause 1, not cause 3** — cause 3 is still a real latent gap
(the HUD never says to hold) but it is not what he hit. Read E9 before acting on this paragraph.
It reloads as a held per-shell chain (`$ALCD` mag 1→6 at ~420 ms per shell while the handle is down) —
**F128**, parked in `docs/post-mvp.md:484`. A yank feeds nothing and the HUD never says to hold.
Proof: all five `reload partial: N → M of 6 (timeout)` are a chain cut short, and **Tony's shotgun
reserve read exactly 24 — the spawn value — in all five dry-pull nags across the whole of match 3. He
never completed a single shotgun reload that match.** His three `mag still 0` at 20:31:33/36/38 are
followed by `20:31:39 ☠ down`. **F128 should move out of post-MVP** — three players carried the shotgun.

**cause 4 (~5%):** nothing reacts to a failed reload. `reloadOutcome` is written at `ammo.js:533` and read
only by the debug panel (`engine.js:7539`). That is **F277** verbatim.

**Answered along the way:** `$AMMO` re-send is **not** the cause — the protocol rule applies to a
mid-match `$WEAP` re-push, not an ALT swap, and MC already puts `$AMMO,0` and `$AMMO,1` in both spawn
and revive bursts (`compile.py:2660-2667`). Reserves were **not** exhausted (Tony's nags read 48, 41,
96, 72, 108… and were decrementing). `mag still 0` repeats because `reloadPulled` (`ammo.js:482`) opens
the takeover on the **guessed** slot; if the gun is really on the other slot and that slot is full it
ignores the lever and emits no `$ALCD` at all → timeout → identical wrong guess next yank.

**Fold together:** F542 (re-rank P0) · F445 (*"open, not reproduced"* — **now reproduced 26×**) ·
F277 · F379 · F128 (to MVP) · F229.

**Bench, cheapest first:**
1. **ALT swallow matrix (~20 min, highest value).** Two slots loaded, full capture. Press ALT, pull

### 4. Phones needed the QR — **MC minted a new install identity at launch**

`~/.brx-mcp/mc-install-secret` is stamped **Oct 17 13:17 — MC's own launch time** — and
`mc-trust-enrolled.txt` holds 13 lines, all from today, each at that node's first hello. So MC created
a brand-new install identity at startup, **invalidating every trust key every phone held from previous
sessions**. That is the whole of this report.

`load_install_secret` (`mcp/brx_mcp/mc/mcid.py`) creates a new secret on `FileNotFoundError` via
**`except FileNotFoundError: pass` — silently**; only the malformed/unreadable branches log. So an
event that forces a tap or QR on *every* phone happened with no log line, no banner, no operator
notice. **Why the file was missing at 13:17 the bundle cannot answer** — needs the laptop's own history
(`~/.brx-mcp` itself is old; `armory.json` is Sep 1).

The field sequence (`p0/mc-connect.log`, PL1's third phone, device clock UTC):
```
20:00:39  MC address remembered — connecting: ws://<lan-ip-1>:8766/ws   <- stale; MC was on <lan-ip-2>
20:00:49  MC connect: no welcome within 10000 ms          -> sweep starts
20:01:06  MISSION CONTROL AT <lan-ip-2>:8766 DID NOT PROVE IT IS YOURS (bad_proof)
20:01:06  UNVERIFIED MISSION CONTROL · TAP JOIN IF YOURS
20:02:01  joining the Mission Control found on the LAN    <- human tap, 55 s later
```

**Verdicts:**
- Requiring a tap when MC cannot prove itself is the **intended security property**
  (`mcid.py`: *"that is the safe failure"*). **Do not weaken it.**
- **Bug 1:** the identity rotation is invisible. Log it and surface it once —
  *"NEW MC IDENTITY: EVERY PHONE WILL NEED ONE TAP OR THE QR."*
- **Bug 2 (diagnostic):** `transport.js:875-877` returns `bad_proof` for four different situations
  including an **empty key list**. The `no_key` code already exists at `:212` and is never set, so the
  phone accuses the genuine MC of being an impostor when the truth is "MC's identity changed".
- Clearing app storage produces the *`new`* row, not `bad_proof` — so that phone had **not** had its
  storage cleared at that moment; it held a key from the old install.

**The stale address cost ~10 s, not 82 s.** It is never invalidated (`app.js:425` deliberately returns
to it) but does not block discovery — the sweep runs in parallel after the 10 s welcome window.
Measured: 27 s mechanical + **55 s waiting for a human**. Worst case to *find* MC is 10 s + ~145 s (six
subnets × ~24 s), then **unbounded** if the proof fails, because `VERIFY_COOLDOWN_MS = 10 min`
(`autojoin.js:27`) blocks further automatic dials.
Cheap wins: kick `sweepForMc()` **alongside** the first remembered dial; mark a remembered address cold
after 2 missed welcomes.

**Flagged, needs one clean launch to confirm:** `mc.log:12` says mDNS is advertising
(`_openbrx._tcp`), yet **no device log contains a single discovery or mdns line all session** — every
join was QR, tap, remembered or sweep. Working mDNS would make discovery ~1 s instead of 16-145 s.

#### ❗ Correction to an earlier theory in **investigation-log.md** (in the bundle)
`persisted context unreadable … reading 'matchId'` (4×) does **not** mint new node_ids and is **not** a
crash. It is a misreport of a normal TTL expiry: `engine.js:1125` logs inside `_load()`, which runs
inside the Engine constructor (`engine.js:771`), while `app/src/app.js:37-39`'s logger starts with
`const mid = engine.matchId;` — esbuild hoists that `const` to `var`, so `engine` is `undefined`

log calls are already `queueMicrotask`-deferred for exactly this reason; `:1125` was missed. Confirming
negative: `persisted context expired` appears **0 times** in all 12 device logs.
`node_id` lives in a different key owned by `transport.js:483-492`; `mc.log` has **zero**
`storage reset` warnings and the per-node seq ranges are contiguous with different starting points
(`fea965017b` 1061-1204, `59adc48191` 738-1214) — **three different phone installs.**
**Issue 1 caused the phone swapping; the phone swapping did not cause issue 1.**
Fix: `app.js:39` → `const mid = engine && engine.matchId;` and defer `engine.js:1125`.

### 6. Melee range — one token, and we control it

Every melee frame shipped identically in all four matches: `$WEAP,4,1,90,13,1,90,0,…` — **token 2
(range) = 90**.

`protocol/brx-protocol.md` §6 has this bench-measured (F231/F234) and traced to a formula: range is a
**carrier frequency, not a power** — a low value detunes the carrier out of the receiver's ~38 kHz
band-pass; emitter power never moves with range. **A melee word is emitted by the HEADSET**, which has
the steeper slope **`38000 − 140 × (100 − range)` Hz**. And: *"above the knee every value is inside the
pass-band, so the differences we ship there are probably not felt."*

At token 2 = 90 the carrier is **36.6 kHz** — comfortably inside the pass-band, i.e. full gun-like
range. That is why melee reached 15-20 ft, and why dropping it to 50 or 60 would likely change nothing.

| token 2 | headset carrier | |
|---|---|---|
| 90 (today) | 36.60 kHz | full range |
| 70 | 33.80 kHz | still inside |
| 50 | 31.00 kHz | approaching |
| **40** | **29.60 kHz** | **≈ the knee — derived, not measured** |
| 30 | 28.20 kHz | doc reference point |
| 13 | 25.82 kHz | doc reference point |

⚠️ The knee is bench-measured only for the **gun barrel** (≈29.4 kHz at t2 = 31). The ~40 row is that

ladder; it is a different emitter. One-token change on slot 4.

## Appendix E10 — issue 2, the two-pistol case: the same cause 1, and a UI tell nobody had named

Tony, on match 4 (`3322a9cf49`), carrying **USP in slot 0** (clip 12 / reserve 120) and **Desert Eagle
in slot 1** (clip 7 / reserve 48):

> *"it felt like alt sometimes didnt switch. it would sometimes fire deagle and sometimes not. it would
> sometimes reload it and sometimes not. and **alt would sometimes switch back to the other pistol after
> reload**"*

That last clause names a symptom that was in the logs the whole time without anyone recognising it.

### The sequence (`devices/node-c8fc56b8b2.events.log`, UTC; local = −4 h)

```

20:48:45  swap to slot 0 assumed after 500ms (no shot yet)
20:48:46  swap to slot 1 assumed after 500ms (no shot yet)

20:48:59  swap to slot 0 assumed after 500ms (no shot yet)
```

### `reload moved from slot N to gun slot M` **is** "ALT switched back after reload"

`app/src/ammo.js:329`. The phone opened its reload takeover believing slot 0; the gun's `$ALCD` then
reported slot **1**; the phone moved its bookkeeping to match. `ammo.js:384/394` set `altPtr` and
`activeSlot` from that `$ALCD` — so **the HUD snaps to the gun's truth**, and the player sees the
weapon change by itself right after reloading.

**It is the correction working, and it surfaces to the player as a bug.** Nobody had connected that
log line to a user-visible symptom; it reads like bookkeeping. It is the single clearest
player-facing tell of cause 1, and the only one that is *positive* evidence (the gun told us its slot)
rather than inferred.

### The rest of his sentence, line by line

| his words | the log |
|---|---|
| "alt sometimes didnt switch" | 74 assumed swaps vs **4** confirmed on this phone; the gun swallows presses (3 bench-proven conditions, cause 1) |
| "sometimes fire deagle and sometimes not" | pulls land on whichever slot the **gun** is really on, which is not always the HUD's |
| "sometimes reload it and sometimes not" | reserve **48 → 41** is one real deagle reload (clip 7); the 20:49:05 and 20:49:11 timeouts are two that fed nothing |
| "switch back to the other pistol after reload" | `reload moved from slot 0 to gun slot 1`, twice (20:48:58, 20:49:28) |

**The reserve numbers are the forensic trail.** 48 and 41 are deagle (reserve 48, clip 7); 96 and 72
are USP (reserve 120, clip 12, so 120→96→72 is four completed USP reloads). **Reloads were landing the
whole time — on the USP — while he was trying to reload the deagle.**

### Two things this adds

1. **A shorter swap window makes divergence more likely.** Both pistols ship `$WEAP` t15 = 500, and
   `compile.py:2714` takes `max(t15)` of the loaded slots, so the assumed-swap commits after **500 ms**
   here against 850-1105 ms elsewhere. The phone commits its guess sooner, giving the gun less time to
   actually move. Worth a column in the bench matrix (test #2): run it at 500 and at 1105.
2. **`reload moved from slot…` should be promoted to a player-facing signal, not a log line.** Whatever
   the eventual cause 1 fix, the HUD currently changes the player's weapon with no explanation. Until the
   slot is authoritative, say so: *"GUN WAS ON <WEAPON>"*.
