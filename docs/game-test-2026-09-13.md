## Block A — the three criticals, all root-caused from the store

### A1 · F206 · TEAM MODES REGISTER NOTHING. 116 shots, zero hits.
**Symptom** (Tony). "we tested a TDM game, we cant damage each other. still critical issue. teams dont work"

**Evidence.** Match 2 ran 42 s and recorded **not one `hit_taken` and not one `death`** on either node, while
the status stream counted **116 shots fired** (ROCCO 103, TONY 13). Teams were pushed correctly — ROCCO
`$TID,1`, TONY `$TID,2`, and both node logs say `advertising as player N team N`. Both FFA matches, on the
same guns, in the same half hour, registered hits normally.

**Mechanism.** The one variable across the three heads is `$GSET` **token 1, `friendlyFire`**:

```
match 1 ffa indoor   $GSET,1,0,1,0,1,0,50,1,*   FF ON    28 hits
match 2 tdm outdoor  $GSET,0,0,1,0,1,0,50,1,*   FF OFF    0 hits
match 3 ffa outdoor  $GSET,1,0,1,0,1,0,50,1,*   FF ON    10 hits
```

`compile.py:978` is `friendly_fire=(config["mode"] == "ffa")`, so **every non-FFA mode we ship sends t1=0**,
and t1=0 is the state in which no shot from anybody registers on anybody.

That is **F49** reproduced at game scale. F49 (bench, 2026-09-07) found the same polarity backwards: with FF
off, a shot claiming the gun's **own** team registered and a shot claiming the **enemy** team did not. The
reading both observations fit: **t1=1 disables the team gate entirely ("register everything"), and t1=0
registers only a MATCHING team** — and something in the emitted word never matches. Every `hit_taken` in this
session carries **`shooter_team: 0`** while both guns were on `$TID,1` or `$TID,2`, so the emitted team is 0
and the receiver's TID is never 0. Candidate for where that 0 comes from: `$PSET` token 2, which
`compile.py` hardcodes to 0 for every player (`$PSET,1,0,45,120,...`).

**Fix direction.** Do not guess the polarity — measure it. Cheapest decisive rung: push a TDM head with
`friendly_fire=True` and opposite TIDs, and shoot. If hits land, ship team modes with t1=1 and score friendly
fire away server-side (MC knows both teams from `$HIR`, and **Q13** already says a team-blocked shot is
invisible on the wire, so gating in the gun buys us nothing we cannot do better in MC). Second rung: vary
`$PSET` t2 with `$TID` fixed and see which one the receiver compares against.

### A2 · F207 · `GUN ECHO ≠ CONFIG` IS A FALSE POSITIVE. It blocked START on healthy guns.
**Symptom** (Tony). "2 guns cannot start … ▲ GUN ECHO ≠ CONFIG (WEAPON 8/24 echoed vs 8/48 expected,
mag/reserve)" and again "36/108 vs 36/216 … 14/28 vs 14/56 … not sure why this error"

**Evidence.** All six `ack_config` rows, against the `$WEAP,0` frame in the head MC actually pushed:

| weapon | head `$WEAP,0` t16/t17 | …and its t39/t40 tail | gun echoed |
|---|---|---|---|
| sniper_rifle + extended_mags | `8,48` | `8,24` | `$ALCD,8,100,0,24,0,*` |
| amr | `14,56` | `14,28` | `$ALCD,14,100,0,28,0,*` |
| burst_rifle | `36,216` | `36,108` | `$ALCD,36,100,0,108,0,*` |

**Mechanism.** The magazine is right every time; the reserve is **exactly half** every time, and that half is
not an approximation — it is **the t40 value MC itself wrote into the same frame**. `compile.py` maintains the
captured frame's invariant `tok17 == 2 * tok40` (`put("reserve", reserve); put("reserve_half", reserve // 2)`).
The gun's `$ALCD` reserve field mirrors **t40**, not t17. `frames.py` reads `$ALCD` index 4 and compares it to
`_WEAP_RESERVE = 18` (t17), so **the comparison can never pass on any weapon on any gun**, and
`state.py _refuse_echo_mismatch` then refuses START.

This also answers **F201** ("every gun echoed 96 against a compiled reserve of 192 — does the gun clamp, or
halve?"). Neither: 96 was t40. Four weapons now agree.

**Fix.** Compare `$ALCD` reserve against `t17 // 2` (equivalently, against the head's own t40), or compare
`2 x $ALCD reserve` against t17. One line in `frames.alcd_ammo`'s caller, plus a test with a real captured
head — `test_mc_config_proof` currently proves this check against MC's own synthetic frames, which is exactly
why it stayed green. Shipping the refusal as FORCEABLE was what saved the session; F201's question of whether
it can become force-proof is now answerable **yes, once the comparison is right**.

**Still open, and it needs a gun.** This says the echo check reads the wrong field. It does **not** say how
many rounds the player actually has — see F215.

### A3 · F208 · A GUN CAN DIE WHILE THE HUD HOLDS THE PLAYER ALIVE, AND NOTHING CAN RECOVER IT.
**Symptom** (Tony). "8:50 into ffa game. i hit rocco, he got deathscream and headset green flash. hud thinks
he is alive. he cant shoot. we had no way to recover the hud to get in sync. we had to stop the game."

**Evidence.** Match 3, and the store has the whole thing.

```
18:47:34  hit_taken  ROCCO  dmg=45 sensor=4      <- the last event of the match
18:47:35  status     ROCCO  alive=True hp=45 armor=5 ammo=36 shots=25 src=gun
   ...    status     ROCCO  IDENTICAL, every 2 s, for 105 s
18:49:20  status     ROCCO  alive=True hp=45 armor=5 ammo=36 shots=25 src=gun
18:49:21  match ended by the operator
```

ROCCO's node log for the same moment shows the armour readout animating down and blinking — and **no `☠ down`
line**, where the earlier death at 18:46:51 logged `☠ down — by TONY` normally. So the phone booked the 45
damage as a hit and kept him alive.

Meanwhile the gun was **not** dead on the link: the frame ring shows `$BUT,0,1` / `$BUT,0,0` pairs still
arriving at 18:48:36–18:48:44 and 18:49:09–18:49:11. **Rocco was pulling the trigger and nothing fired** —
`shots` never moved off 25. The gun link finally dropped at 18:49:20 (`*** gun disconnected ***`).

**Mechanism, in two halves.**
1. The gun's pool and the phone's pool disagreed, and the phone had no way to notice. `pool_src` said `gun`
   throughout, yet the value was 105 s stale. **There is no staleness detector on the pool anywhere** — a gun
   that has said nothing for two minutes is indistinguishable from a healthy idle one, on the HUD and on the
   MC board alike.
2. The phone's end-of-match state has `alive:false, respawnIn:0, respawnGate:"trigger"` in a match configured
   `respawn {type:"auto", delay_s:15}` — see **F212**. If the phone had him gated on a trigger pull, the
   `$BUT` frames above show the pull arriving and being ignored.

**Fix direction.** Three separable pieces, none needing a gun to write:
(a) a **pool-staleness watchdog** — if the gun has streamed no `$ALCD`/`$PSET`-derived pool change for N
seconds while `$BUT` traffic continues, the HUD says SO rather than showing a confident stale number;
(b) an **operator resync** — MC can re-push a player's head and `$SPAWN` mid-match. Today the only exits are
RE-PUSH (whole lobby) and ending the game, which is what happened;
(c) settle F212 so a trigger-gated respawn cannot appear under an auto-respawn config.

## Block B — respawn, and what it does to a life

### B1 · F209 · THE RESPAWN DELAY COLLAPSES TO ZERO IN A BURST, AND SPAWN PROTECTION DOES NOT HOLD.
**Symptom** (Tony). "you can die and get shot before the weapon comes online during respawn"

**Evidence.** Match 1 (respawn `delay_s: 5`). The ordinary cadence is clean — death, +5.0 s, respawn. Twice
it is not:

```
18:38:17  ROCCO  hit death respawn hit death respawn hit death respawn hit death respawn hit death respawn
18:40:34  TONY   hit death respawn hit death respawn hit death respawn hit death
```

Five deaths and five respawns inside **one second**, every respawn at **+0.0 s after its death**, in a game
whose respawn delay is 5 s. And outside the bursts, hits land at **+1.6 s, +2.9 s, +3.0 s** after a respawn.

**Mechanism (candidates, not settled).** Nothing here has a refractory period: a burst of `$HIR` frames (an IR
flood at close range, or a queue flushing) is processed as N independent hit→death→respawn cycles, and the
delay is skipped rather than queued. For the +1.6/+2.9/+3.0 s hits: **F121/A23** shipped spawn protection —
the head carries a no-pool `$SIR` table and the live table rides `$SPAWN`/`revive`. Its bench gate has never
been run, and this is the first field look at it. Either the protection is not landing, or it ends the instant
`$SPAWN` is written and the weapon takes ~2 s longer than that to come online (the node log shows `write gun
take` ~3 s after `respawned`, then `swap to slot 1 assumed after 850ms`). The asymmetry is the bug Tony
reported: **hit reception returns before the trigger does.**

**Fix direction.** A per-life refractory on the death path (one death per life, subsequent `$HIR` discarded
until the respawn completes), and a spawn-protection window that ends when the **weapon** is armed, not when
`$SPAWN` is sent. Both are keyboard work; the proof needs two guns.

### B2 · F212 · `respawnGate: "trigger"` UNDER AN `auto` RESPAWN CONFIG.
Both phones end both of the last two matches with `respawnType:"auto"`, `respawnIn:0` and
`respawnGate:"trigger"`, against configs that say `respawn {type:"auto", delay_s:15}`. Either the gate is
mislabelled or a trigger-gated respawn is reachable in a mode that never asked for one — and A3 is what that
costs when it goes wrong. Read `engine.js`'s respawn gate against `contracts.md`'s respawn table; no hardware
needed to decide which of the two it is.

### B3 · F216 · THE HEADSET PLAYED THE DEAD/AWAITING-RESPAWN FLASH WHILE ALIVE.
**Symptom** (Tony). "about 8:30 on Tony in this session my headset leds started flashing like i was dead and
wating to respawn but i was alive and fine"

Not isolated in the store — no envelope marks it, and the node logs write headset frames constantly. The two
things that make it plausible rather than mysterious are in this sheet already: the **burst** in B1 (five
death/respawn pairs in a second will leave any LED state machine behind) and the **desync** in A3 (the phone
and the gun disagreeing about alive). Treat it as a symptom of one of those until it reproduces on its own.
`eyes`, but look at B1 and A3 first.

## Block D — ammo, and two things that are not the same bug

### D1 · F215 · THE SNIPER'S EXTENDED-MAGS AMMO READOUT DOES NOT MATCH THE GUN.
**Symptom** (Tony). "bug with extended mags" · "sniper rifle extended mags ammo indicator on hud lies. it
isn't accurate with the number"

**What is certain.** `extended_mags` is `ammo_mult: 2` and `_mods` scales **both** mag and reserve, so the
sniper's 4/24 compiles to 8/48 — and the gun echoed `8, …, 24`. Per **F207** that 24 is the head's own t40 and
proves nothing about the gun's real reserve. So there are two readings and the store cannot separate them:
either the doubling landed and the HUD is showing the right number (48) while the echo field shows half, or
the doubling landed on the magazine only and the player really does have 24. Tony's ear is on the second.

**The test, and it is one minute at the bench.** Push the sniper with `extended_mags`, fire the gun dry
counting rounds, and compare against 48. That single count settles F207's residue, F215 and **A10c**
(*"Extended Mags HUD max matches the `$AMMO,0` we write (64/768)"*, never run) at once. `trigger`.

**Worth checking in the same pass:** ROCCO's phone ended match 3 holding `reserve: 201` against
`loadReserve: 216` with `mag: 6` on a **shotgun** whose own reserve is 24 — the HUD's reserve appears to track
the primary while the secondary is in hand.

### D2 · F213 · THE HUD SHOWS ARMOUR ABOVE ITS OWN MAXIMUM.
Both phones, matches 2 and 3: `"armor":120, "maxArmor":70`. The `body_armor` perk's `max_armor_add: 50` is
baked into the pushed `$PSET` (120) but `maxArmor` is still the game's 70, so every armour bar and every
percentage that divides by it is wrong for anyone carrying the perk. Keyboard work; `compile.py` already knows
the real ceiling, it just is not sent as the max.
