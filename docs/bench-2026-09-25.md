## MUST items not run as written, and why (and what was proven without Tony)

- **Proven overnight without Tony (brx4, 2026-09-25, Stick firmware `e17efa66`; evidence in the experiment log), so
  not re-run:** Block 9 step 1, **mDNS**: the Stick found and joined an MC run natively on Windows Python (a
  WSL-hosted MC cannot be discovered, because of the portproxy). The A58 status fields and the lock round trip (Block 9
  step 6 except the physical button): LOAD locks, a reconnect re-sends the remaining lock, UNLOCK STATIONS unlocks, the
  PMIC bits read 2b/01 locked and 2a/00 unlocked, and serial writes are refused while locked. The **offline pickup
  award** (the core of Block 10 step 2(b)) with a simulated player advert: the Stick awarded the claim with no MC,
  and refused a dead player's claim. Block 9 step 7's Stick half: advert gaps median 106 ms, max 953 ms while scanning,
  none over 1 s, so the phones' "left at -54" flips are phone-side scanning.
- **MC and the Stick tomorrow:** keep MC in WSL and type the Stick's MC address (below), OR run MC natively on
  Windows so mDNS works. Never both: the Windows portproxy holds ports 8765 and 8766.
- **No revive steps: revive counting is post-MVP.** Tony, 2026-09-24: "lets remove the revive count for now and we
  can add those post mvp". A respawn Stick now only advertises (no scan, no count, no REDEPLOY;
  `REVIVE_FEEDBACK_ENABLED` off), and the phone's revive bit is parked on branch `revive-bit`. Block 10 step 2 (the
  pickup) replaces Block 9 steps 2 and 5.
- **7.11: watched, not re-run.** It passed on 2026-09-24 (10/10 at about 2 m). Sitting C only watches for the in-fight
  corruption; re-run 7.11 at 1 m and 5 m only if a wrong magnitude shows up.
