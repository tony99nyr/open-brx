## Block A — confirmed, cheap, one rebuild covers all six

### A1 · F110 · Briefing title and description are sheared off at the bottom
**Symptom.** "FREE FOR ALL text cut off at bottom; the mode description cut off at the bottom on the
second line" (iPhone).
**Evidence.** Measured with Playwright at 891×411, 667×375 **and** 812×375 — clips at all three:

```
.bfname   content 57px -> box 35px   clipped   (46px type, 42.32px line-height, max-height 88px)
.bfdesc   content 54px -> box 30px   clipped   (2 lines need 36.25px, got 30px)
.bfbody   overflows: false
```

**Mechanism.** `www/index.html:629` — `.bf .bfbody` is a **fixed-height flex column**
(`top:26px; bottom:104px`). Flex items shrink by default, so `.bfname` / `.bfdesc` are squeezed
*below their own content height* and their own `overflow:hidden` shears the text. The body never
overflows, which is why no overflow check caught it.
**Refuted.** Not the `@media (max-height:360px)` rescue at `:650`. The HUD renders into a **fixed
844×390 `#frame`** that is then scaled, so that query keys off the device viewport and is irrelevant
to the layout. Device-independent.
**Fix.** `flex-shrink:0` on `.bf .r` rows, and/or size the type to the box.
**Test gap.** `app/tools/screens.mjs` never asserts `scrollHeight > clientHeight` on these.

### A2 · F117 · The post-match button does not read as a control
**Symptom.** "MC waits for all players to be ready, but it is NOT obvious that you need to click the
'Match complete' button to get ready. very confusing."
**Evidence.** `app/src/hud/hud.js` — the same `onReady` control, styled two ways:

```
225 kitted: <button class="ready off"  data-act="onReady">  READY UP        imperative
228 over:   <button class="ready wait" data-act="onReady">  MATCH COMPLETE  declarative
231 armed:  <button class="ready wait" (NO data-act)      >  STANDING BY     genuinely INERT
```

`www/index.html:378-379` — `.ready.off{color:var(--num);font-size:26px}` vs
`.ready.wait{color:var(--mut);font-size:20px}`.
**Mechanism.** Four independent signals all say "not a control" on the one button gating the next
match: the label states a fact; muted `--mut` grey; 20 px against the pre-match button's 26 px; and
the **identical classes to the inert STANDING BY button**. This inverts the repo's own rule in
`.claude/skills/ui-build-verify/SKILL.md` ("if a tap legitimately does nothing… it should not look
tappable").
**Fix.** Imperative label ("READY FOR NEXT MATCH ▸"), the `off` treatment not `wait`, and lead the
note with the instruction instead of burying it in sentence two.

### A3 · F122 · Debug panel: scroll resets, taps are eaten, SHARE LOG never sends
**Symptom.** "scroll to the bottom, after a second it jumps up and you can't hit the share log
button. took 6 tries." Then: pressed SHARE LOG **multiple times on the iPhone** and nothing arrived.
**Evidence.** 9 × `log_offer` + 9 × `log_data` in the store, **all** from the Android; **zero** from
the iPhone.
**Mechanism.** `app/src/app.js:383` `renderNow()` calls `hud.setDiag(...)` on **every** render;
`hud.js:117` re-renders when open; `hud.js:731` does `this.diag.innerHTML = ...` — a full DOM
replacement. State updates land ~1 s apart, so:
1. `scrollTop` resets to 0 every second, and
2. a tap whose `touchstart` and `touchend` straddle a re-render **produces no `click` at all**,
   because the element under the finger was destroyed. Pressing ten times can yield zero clicks.

SHARE LOG is the worst-hit control: the `.btns` row is last, after the `LOG` `<pre>`.
⚠ **This cost us evidence tonight** — SHARE LOG is the only way to flush the BLE frame ring, and it
is the button the bug makes unreachable. The panel is also, by definition, where you go when
something is already wrong.
**Fix.** (a) preserve `scrollTop`, or skip the write when the HTML is unchanged; (b) **move the
button row into a sticky footer** — they are all actions and none should need scrolling past a
growing log.
**Test gap.** No step scrolls a panel and asserts position survives a re-render.

### A4 · F126 · iPhone debug panel font is inflated
**Symptom.** "on iphone the debug menu font size is super large. idk why."
**Evidence.** `-webkit-text-size-adjust` is **not set anywhere** in `app/www/index.html`.
`#diag` (`index.html:433`) is `font:11px/1.5 ui-monospace` with `overflow:auto`, i.e. a scroller,
inside the scaled `#frame`.
**Mechanism.** WKWebView auto-inflates text in scrollable blocks unless the property is pinned.
Android is unaffected, which matches the report.
**Fix.** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%` on `html,body` (and/or `#diag`). One line.
**Note.** Tony's link to A3 is right: taller text pushes SHARE LOG further down. They compound.

**Symptom.** "the animation of the large numbers when DOWN animates weird. the numbers kinda get cut

**Evidence.** Measured at 812×375 on `?demo&stage=down`:

### B1 · F124 · The frag limit is never enforced 🔴
**Symptom.** "the player got the 7th kill after the 'next kill wins' audio and the game didnt end."
**Evidence.** Live MC: `scoring {"frag_limit": 7, "win_by": "kills"}`, ROCCO **kills=9**, phase still
`live`.
**Mechanism.** The end exists in the **standalone CLI mode engine** and was never ported to MC:

```
mcp/brx_mcp/modes/deathmatch.py:110   if self.config.frag_limit and self.team_score[killer_team] >= self.config.frag_limit:
mcp/brx_mcp/mc/scoring.py:373         if self.frag_limit and best == self.frag_limit - 1 ...   # next_kill_wins ONLY
```

Not enforced node-side either (`engine.js:2379` exposes `fragLimit` for display only) nor gun-side
(`gameconfig.py:286` declares `frag_limit` and **nothing ever reads or emits it**). So the limit is
configured, announced at cap−1, displayed, and never enforced. Matches end only on `time_limit_s`
or a manual END.
**The intent is documented.** `compile.py:1434-1436` warns "frag_limit on a non-full-coverage venue
is an in-coverage **early end** only".
**Fix.** End in `Scorer` when `best >= frag_limit`, through the same path `control('end')` uses
(`scorer.set_end()` + `state._finish()`).

Last BLE frame `t=1789173918006`; the `ends_t` captured from `/api/state` was `1789173918017`. MC

**Suspect mechanism.** `mcp/brx_mcp/mc/state.py:2003-2021` — the node push loop sets `reached`

```python
2015:  if cmd == "end" and self.scorer:

2018:  else:                                          # comment says "recall/panic"

```

If `self.scorer` is `None` when `end` arrives, END falls into the **recall/panic** branch: nodes are

**Fix.** Make the reported count mean "ended", not "pushed"; handle `end` with no scorer explicitly.
**Note.** An earlier early-end in the same session **did** work (see "What worked"), so this is
conditional, not a flat failure.

### B3 · F116 · Medals never reach the score row, and the wrong streak is shown
**Symptom.** "MC match page doesn't show any medals at all."
**Evidence.** `TONY kills 9 deaths 8 kd 1.12 streak 0 medals []` · `ROCCO … streak 8 medals []` ·
`recap.honors: []` · 2 scored players. Yet the **feed** shows `tag: "FIRST BLOOD"` and
`tag: "STREAK ×3"`, so the medals really are computed at event time.
**Three causes.**
1. **Honors need 3+ players.** `row["medals"]` comes entirely from `medals()` → `honors()`
   (`scoring.py:549-553`), which hard-returns `[]` below three scored players (`:558`). Deliberate
   (design review 2026-08-26 #3: "a 1-player recap crowned itself MVP"). **In any 1v1 it is always
   empty.**
2. **The Halo per-kill medals never reach the row.** `scoring.py:298-312` computes `first_blood`,
   `double_kill`, `triple_kill`, `killtacular`, `killing_spree`, `unstoppable`, fires them as A11.4
   alerts (`:332`) and **discards them**. Even at 8 players the page would show only
   MVP/MOST KILLS/SURVIVOR.
3. **The row exposes the wrong streak.** `ScoreRow.streak` is the **current** streak, ~0 for whoever
   died last — hence "9 kills, streak 0". The scorer already tracks `best_streak` (`:285`),
   `multi_best` (`:295`) and `first_blood` (`:300`) and exposes **none** of them.
**Fix.** Surface `first_blood` / `multi_best` / `best_streak` into `rows()`. All three are already
computed; no new tracking. This gives real medals even in a 1v1 where `honors()` is empty by design.

### B5 · F119 · ACC swings wildly
**Symptom.** "ACC on the MC match page is wildly inaccurate at times. we probably need to show a
little animation until it settles."
**Mechanism.** Numerator and denominator arrive on **different clocks**:

```
hits  -> Scorer.ingest_batch   (event facts)          IMMEDIATE, per event
shots -> Scorer.ingest_status  (status heartbeat)     SAMPLED ~2 s APART   (scoring.py:188)
accuracy = 100 * hits / (shots_baseline + shots)                           (scoring.py:392-396)
```

Land 2–3 hits between heartbeats and ACC spikes; it can exceed 100 %. Same ~2 s sampling trap the Mac
runbook already documents ("ammo never reached 0").
**Two things make the fix easy.** `scoring.py:576` already refuses SHARPSHOOTER below **10 shots**, so
the small-sample precedent exists; and `st.shots_t` records when shots were last sampled, so "the
denominator is stale" is **detectable**, not guessed. MC also already has recap-level `provisional`
(`state.py:2088`) and `settling` (`:2121`) — there is no *per-metric* settling concept, which is the
gap.
**Fix.** Suppress or mark ACC provisional until `shots >= 10`, or while `shots_t` predates the last hit.

### B6 · F127 · CONTINUE to lobby locks out everyone still kitting 🔴
**Symptom.** "if MC hits continue and goes to lobby it messes with everyone actively kitting. Seems
fine to MC but locks everyone out. There should be a warning and a status of how many people readied."
**MC's own rule says this should not be possible.** `mcp/brx_mcp/mc/state.py:540-548`, `_on_ready`:

> *"Shared ready semantics (§4.4): ready ENDS that player's try-out … and **kit → lobby advances only
> when EVERY rostered player is ready**."*

```python
if self.phase == "kit" and ready and self._all_ready():
    self.phase = "lobby"
```

**Mechanism — the UI bypasses it.** `webapp/mc/src/screens/Kit.tsx:193`:

```jsx
<PrimaryButton onClick={async () => { await run(() => api.setPhase('lobby')); setView('lobby'); }}>CONTINUE ▸</PrimaryButton>
```

`api.setPhase('lobby')` is a **bare phase set** with no readiness check and no confirm, so it skips
`_all_ready()` entirely. There are two routes to lobby and CONTINUE takes the unguarded one: the other,
`POST /api/lobby/push`, **does** refuse on reds and names each blocker unless forced (`mc/API.md`).
**Why players are locked out.** On the node, `engine.js:454`
(`if (this.phase !== 'armed' && this.phase !== 'live') this._set('lobby')`) moves the node to `lobby`,
and `hud.js:135` then renders the lobby screen instead of the kit panel — a player mid-loadout loses
the screen out from under them, with nothing said on either side.
**The warning data already exists.** `state.py:1627-1628` computes
`{"roster_size": len(board), "greens": greens, ...}` — literally "how many people readied". It is
computed today and simply never shown on that button.
**Fix.** Show `greens / roster_size` on the CONTINUE button; warn (or two-step confirm) when
`greens < roster_size`, naming who is not ready; and prefer the guarded push path over a bare
`setPhase`. Node side: decide what a player mid-kit should see rather than being silently moved.

## Block C — confirmed node-side

### C1 · F121 · Hit reception is armed at config push and never disarmed 🔴
**Symptom.** "during the countdown you can take damage apparently. while team colors are still on
headsets." And separately: "you can get hit by shots before the gun is armed during spawn."
**These are one bug.** `mcp/brx_mcp/mc/compile.py:934`:

```python
head += sir_rows + bmap + gc._led_frames() + hled + gun_pre + [f"$TID,{tid},*"]
#       ^^^^^^^^                              ^^^^   ^^^^^^^
#       arms HIT RECEPTION                    pregame team colour (headset + gun body)
```

Per CLAUDE.md's F11 rule a gun with **no `$SIR` table cannot be hit** — so the `$SIR` table *is* the
arming of hit reception, and it ships in the **same frame list** as the pregame team colour, at
lobby-push time: before the countdown, long before go-live.
⚠ **The team colour on the headset is the visible marker that you have become hittable.** Tony spotted
that the two coincide; they are adjacent entries in one push.
**Software is correctly guarded** (MC's score is unaffected): damage facts are gated on
`phase === 'live' && this.spawned && this.alive` (`engine.js:2069/2084/2104`). **The hardware is not** —
hit sounds, LEDs and the gun's native pool decrements all happen.
**There is no spawn-protection concept anywhere**: grep for `invuln` / `spawn_protect` /
`spawn_shield` finds nothing. `MIN_RESPAWN_S` (3 s, F13) gates the respawn *delay*, not invulnerability.
**Fix mechanism already in-tree.** `compile.py:135`
`_SIR_NO_POOL = frozenset({8,23,24,25,26,27,28,35,31,32,34})` — *"registers a `$HIR`, moves no pool"*.
Ship a **no-pool `$SIR` variant pregame** (hits still register for feedback/telemetry but drain
nothing), then push the real table at `$SPAWN`. **Swapping `$SIR` mid-game is proven safe**: `_revive`
re-sends `$SIR` rows every life (`engine.js:1573`) and the F11 repair path depends on it.

### C2 · F123 · easy_reload fails on the shotgun, and the reload animation is a blind timer
**Symptom.** "easy reload does not work with the shotgun… holding alt fire doesnt work." Then: "the
hud animates reloading, but the gun doesnt actually reload."
**Mechanism — three stacked faults.**
1. **The shotgun's reload is a held per-shell chain.** mag 6, `reload_ms` 400 per shell; native
   firmware behaviour from its captured `$WEAP` ("per-weapon reload chains… reload type (t19)"),
   driven by holding the physical **reload handle (button 2)**. `easy_reload` compiles to
   `$BMAP,1,97` (`compile.py:692`) — remapping **alt-fire** to function 97. A momentary press emits
   one reload event, not a sustained chain.
2. **The node cannot see a held button — and the data is already arriving.** `brx-protocol.md:142`
   documents `state 1 press / 0 release`, and the frame ring proves it on hardware:

   ```
   1789173913298 rx $BUT,0,1,*              <- press
   1789173913311 rx $ALCD,30,100,0,360,0,*  <- the shot
   1789173913522 rx $BUT,0,0,*              <- RELEASE
   ```

   `engine.js:1850-1852` handles **only `state === 1`**. Every release is discarded.
3. ⚠ **The HUD reload animation never checks the gun — not shotgun-specific.**
   `engine.js:1971 _reloadPulled()` **writes nothing to the gun**; it sets
   `this.reloading = {at, ms: max(300, reload_s*1000), slot}` and `reloadingMs()` (`:1988`) is a pure
   timer. Nothing reconciles it against real ammo, so **any** failed reload animates as success.
   `_reloadPulled` was designed as a *mirror* of a physical handle pull ("the takeover"); `easy_reload`
   reuses it as if it were a command.
**Fix.** Handle `$BUT` state 0 so holds are observable; reconcile `reloading` against `$ALCD`/`$LCD`
ammo instead of a blind timer; and decide what `easy_reload` means for a chain-reload weapon —
possibly exclude the pairing, as the loadout policy already excludes `easy_reload` + secondary
(`test_mc_loadout.py:183` carries an "ALT button" hint for "the one pairing the gun can't do").

### C3 · F113 · The gun LED strip freezes at a partial level for the whole death
**Symptom.** "killed with sniper rifle, 2 shots. it took down to 1 led of purple and then dead. while
dead it stayed at 1 purple."
**Not a coding mistake — documented, deliberate behaviour.**

```
engine.js:2179  "A16 §5: death clears the readout — NO gun write here, the strip simply sits
                 wherever the native hit flash left it until the next `_gunTake` blanks it"
engine.js:124   "…death is deliberately hands-off, A16"
```

**What the field proved.** The A16 assumption that the native hit flash leaves the strip somewhere

**Tony's own mechanism, and it matches the code exactly.** "its bc i shot it quickly while the armor

outright… a hit just before it can still be mid-animation when death registers."* Fast kills land
death mid-animation; the animation is cancelled, nothing is written, the strip freezes at whatever
partial level it had reached.
**Interaction with balance.** The bigger the damage per shot the worse it looks — a sniper jumps from
a high level to dead in one step; a 13-hit rifle walks the strip down and lands near-empty.
**Decision needed (overturns a documented call).** (a) blank the strip on death, (b) paint an explicit
dead state, or (c) hand off to `$HLOOP` (the A16 down signal). ⚠ A16 hard rule: **`$HLED,,6` must
NEVER be sent in play.** `mcp/tests/test_led_invariants.py` pins the invariants — keep it green.

### D2 · S22 · Weapon balance: sidearms must not out-kill rifles
Tony: "the sidearms should not kill fast, they should kill slower than rifles"; "rate of fire on the
pistol usp should be quicker to match counter strike. less damage but faster rof."
**Model confirmed exact across all 22 weapons:** `ttk_ms == (htk - 1) * wire.fire_ms`.

```

SIDEARM usp      1600  htk 9  dmg 11 mag 12   <- beats EVERY rifle
```
