# Bench sheet: the MacBook, 2026-09-29 (the iPhone, and anything that needs real mDNS)

Updated: 2026-10-03. One of three independent sheets for today; the index is [`bench-plan.md`](bench-plan.md) →
*Part 3*. The other two are [`bench-standard-2026-09-29.md`](bench-standard-2026-09-29.md) and
[`bench-stick-2026-09-29.md`](bench-stick-2026-09-29.md). How a bench run works with Tony: the
[`bench-session` skill](../.claude/skills/bench-session/SKILL.md). **Read
[`mac-dev-runbook.md`](mac-dev-runbook.md) first**: the setup that is not in git, and the restart-MC-vs-hard-reload
rule.

Why a Mac: MC in WSL is never mDNS-discoverable, so a phone with no saved MC address (11.7) cannot find it there,
and the iPhone build needs Xcode.

**Time:** about 75 min: setup 30 min, steps 1-3 about 45 min.

## Kit

- The MacBook, on the same Wi-Fi as the phones.
- The iPhone X, paired to the Mac for development (the first pairing needs a cable; the `iphone-build` skill).
- One gun with its headset for the iPhone, plus a second gun and the grey Pixel for step 3.
- The grey Pixel 5, over wireless adb from the Mac (`mac-dev-runbook.md` §2b).

## Method (it worked on 2026-09-28; keep it)

- **Drive MC through its API** (`Authorization: Bearer <token>`, `mcp/brx_mcp/mc/API.md`).
- **The Pixel's log over CDP** (`mcp/tools/webview_eval.py "window.brx.log.slice(-40)" --serial <ip:port>`,
  with `BRX_ADB=adb` on the Mac). **The iPhone's log:** its ⓘ DEVELOPER toggle, or Safari's Web Inspector
  (iPhone: Settings > Safari > Advanced > Web Inspector; Mac Safari's Develop menu).
- **Read the connection a phone actually made from MC**: `python -m brx_mcp.mc.diag <session.sqlite>`.

## Setup (about 30 min)

1. **The iPhone build:** run the [`iphone-build` skill](../.claude/skills/iphone-build/SKILL.md) but build from `main` at the 0.4.18 cut's sha
   (`git fetch && git checkout <0.4.18 sha>`), so the iPhone reports 0.4.18 like the Pixels; then `cd app && npm ci`, `npm run ios:setup`, `npm run ios:push -- <device>`). Log the short sha and the
   iOS version (Settings > General > About). The iPhone X tops out at iOS 16, and the app needs Safari 16.2
   (`color-mix()`, `app/README.md`): a phone below 16.2 is a real risk.
2. **The grey Pixel, a true first contact.** `adb connect <ip:port>`, then
   `adb shell pm clear com.openbrx.companion` (**this wipes its app data**, its MC trust key and its saved gun),
   then `adb install -r /home/tony/apk-0.4.18/brx-companion-0.4.18-android-release.apk`. Do this from the WSL box before the Mac sitting (the APK lives there), or copy the APK to the Mac first.
   **Do not open the app yet**: step 2 is the test.
3. Start MC on the Mac from the latest `main`, with `--bench-volume` (the runbook's §2 command):
   `.venv/bin/python -m brx_mcp.mc -v --bench-volume > ~/mc-$(date +%Y%m%d-%H%M).log 2>&1 &`. Log the banner
   and the token. MC runs from `main`.
4. Confirm the Mac advertises MC: `dns-sd -B _openbrx._tcp local.` lists it. This is the control for step 2.

## Steps, in priority order

**1. The IPHONE block (15 min).** Follow [`bench-plan.md`](bench-plan.md) → *IPHONE block*, items 1 to 4: install
and open, BLE connect, a match, the ⓘ DEVELOPER toggle. On the Mac, the iPhone may find MC by mDNS: log whether it
did or whether you typed the address. **Pass:** as that block says; a missing or non-holding DEVELOPER toggle is a
FAIL, not INCONCLUSIVE. Row: B21.

**2. 11.7, first-contact auto-join over real mDNS (10 min).** With MC up and advertising (setup step 4), open the
app on the grey Pixel for the first time since the `pm clear`. **Pass:** MC binds a new node id (not an
older one) within about 3 s, with no tap. Log `preflight.auto_join_ok` too, but it reads true from boot, so it
proves nothing alone;
and the SET MY GUN screen reads MC JOINED before the gun is paired (F422, confirmed in Part 2 on another phone).
Then pair the gun. **Control:** in Part 2 the same phone in WSL read NOT JOINED. That is the no-mDNS case. Row: 11.7
(`bench-2026-09-24.md`).

**3. The iPhone as player 2 (20 min).** A short two-gun TDM through the Mac's MC: the grey Pixel as player 1, the
iPhone as player 2. Run 4.0's loop on the iPhone (first launch in day mode, then night mode; lobby, arm, live,
a death and its death screen, the recap). **Pass:** every screen appears in order on the iPhone, as on the Pixel,
with no stuck or blank state; kills and deaths reach MC from both. Log a screenshot of any defect. Row: B21, and
the iOS half of 4.0.
