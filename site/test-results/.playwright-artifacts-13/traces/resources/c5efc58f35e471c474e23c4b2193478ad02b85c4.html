FAQ

Why won't my gun fire?

Check the headset first: it's off, unpaired (slow rainbow blink) or dropped mid-game, and the firmware locks the trigger. Link it, or re-pair. Second most common: the game hasn't started (pull the reload handle) or you're dead. ✅bench📖official👥community

How do I re-pair a headset?

Headset on, hold its small button, boot the tagger holding RIGHT, wait for "PAIRING MODE", pull the trigger once ("HEADSET CONNECTED") and once more ("device paired"). 👥community

The headset went dark once the game started — is it broken?

No. Team colour shows pre-game only; dark during play is native. Rainbow is the fault state. ✅bench

Can I put custom sounds on the tagger?

Yes. Hold SELECT while powering on with the programming port plugged into a computer; a drive appears with an AUDIO folder — replace the .LTP files (slow: ~1 h per 250 MB). Keep the originals. 📖official👥community

Which phones work with the official app?

Callsign works on Android 10 and older, not on newer Android. On any phone the headset must be linked or the app disconnects. 👥community✅bench

What battery and charger does it take?

A 7.4 V ~2200 mAh Li-ion pack with an 8.4 V two-cell smart charger; the headset charges from any 5 V USB. Optional 6×AA — non-rechargeable only. 📖official👥community

Is the battery polarity really reversed?

Yes — reversed from the usual convention. Meter any replacement pack before plugging it in. 👥community

Why is my gun called Tactix2 again?

Opening the official app resets the owner-set name. Rename it and power-cycle. ✅bench

Why do guns fail after about an hour of hosted play?

"Screamers": a documented failure of taggers left powered and hosted for long sessions, plus a firmware rule that refuses Bluetooth re-pairing below a battery threshold. Power-rest guns, rotate them, keep batteries topped. 👥community✅bench

Do I have to open the gun for JEDGE?

No. It rides on the phone bracket with its own power; stock firmware is never touched. The Open BRX Companion is designed the same way. 👥community

Can I change the grenade's mode from an app?

No — it's set only by the button-hold on the device, and it stays set across power cycles. 👥community✅bench

Where do I get 3D-print files?

There is no public BRX library yet; the reload-button STL circulates in the owners' group. Open BRX is building an open, versioned one. 👥community
