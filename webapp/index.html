Open BRX

Last verified 2026-09-09

This is a manual for the Battle Company BRX laser tagger and headset. It covers the hardware, how to run it, and how to fix it when something goes wrong. It is also open-source software for running games on stock BRX guns from a laptop.

  • Meet the BRX: anatomy, buttons, lights, headset, generations
  • Operating the BRX: quick start, charging, the on-gun menu, pairing
  • Gameplay: weapons, health and damage, native game modes
  • Sound, voice and updates: how audio works, the sound bank, custom sounds, firmware
  • Fix, mod and accessorise: troubleshooting by symptom, repairs, mods
  • Developer reference: the BLE and IR protocol, every command
  • The Open BRX platform: the open-source software
  • Credits and sources
