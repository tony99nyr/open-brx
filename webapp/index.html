Open BRX. The definitive manual for the Battle Company BRX tagger and headset — and an open-source platform that turns stock BRX guns into a fully orchestrated laser-tag system: real game modes, live scoring, objectives, a phone HUD, a laptop mission control. No subscription. No firmware mods. No venue Wi-Fi required.

What the platform does today

3 cards with status badges:

  • Play now, laptop-only ✅bench — python -m brx_mcp play tdm <gun1> <gun2> ran a full Team Deathmatch on two real taggers on 2026-08-25: scoring, respawn, frag limit, correct winner. Everyone stays in the laptop's BLE range (a room, a yard).
  • Mission Control + the phone HUD 🚧under construction — a laptop console that authors the game and a phone on each gun that runs it over field Wi-Fi, so players aren't tied to the laptop's BLE range. Under construction — details when it's been run on a real field.
  • The Companion 🚧under construction — a ~$15 ESP32 rider that reconstructs the gun's own kill flash and killstreak audio over BLE, no phone needed. Under construction — specified, bench kit in hand.
