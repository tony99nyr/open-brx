Open BRX

The definitive manual for the Battle Company BRX tagger and headset — and an open-source platform that turns stock BRX guns into an orchestrated laser-tag system: real game modes, live scoring and objectives from a laptop today, with a phone HUD and a laptop Mission Control under construction. No subscription. No firmware mods. No venue Wi-Fi required.

What the platform does today

  • Play now, laptop-only bench — python -m brx_mcp play tdm <gun1> <gun2> ran a full Team Deathmatch on two real taggers on 2026-08-25: scoring, respawn, frag limit, correct winner. Everyone stays in the laptop's BLE range (a room, a yard). → Getting started with brx-mcp
  • Mission Control + the phone HUD under construction — a laptop console that authors the game and a phone on each gun that runs it over field Wi-Fi. Under construction. → The pieces
  • The Companion under construction — a ~$15 ESP32 rider that reconstructs the gun's own kill flash and killstreak audio over BLE, no phone needed. Under construction. → The pieces
