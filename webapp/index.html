Open BRX

This is the full manual for the Battle Company BRX tagger and headset. It is also an open-source platform. It turns stock BRX guns into one connected laser tag system. You get real game modes, live scoring, and objectives from a laptop today. A phone HUD and a laptop Mission Control are still being built. No subscription. No firmware mods. No venue Wi-Fi needed.

Two doors

  • The Ultimate BRX Manual: everything about the tagger and headset in one place. You get anatomy, pairing, every weapon, the 2,477-sound bank, repairs, and the full BLE protocol. We built it from the official docs, the community, and our own bench. benchofficialcommunity → Manual hub
  • The Open BRX platform: run Team Deathmatch and more on stock guns from a laptop today with brx-mcp. That part is proven on real hardware. The rest is under construction: a mission-control console, a phone HUD per gun, and an ESP32 rider. benchunder construction → The Open BRX platform

Source: docs/README.md, docs/architecture-topology.md

What the platform does today

  • Play now, laptop only bench: python -m brx_mcp play tdm <gun1> <gun2> ran a full Team Deathmatch on two real taggers on 2026-08-25. It handled scoring, respawn, the frag limit, and the correct winner. Everyone stays in the laptop's BLE range (a room, a yard). → Getting started with brx-mcp
  • Mission Control + the phone HUD under construction: a laptop console sets up the game. A phone on each gun then runs it over field Wi-Fi. Under construction. → The pieces · Android test build → Get the app
  • The Companion under construction: a ~$15 ESP32 rider that rebuilds the gun's own kill flash and killstreak audio over BLE. You need no phone for it. Under construction. → The pieces
