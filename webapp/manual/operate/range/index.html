Range & Line of Sight

What the numbers on the box mean once the sun comes out.

✅bench📖official👥communityLast verified 2026-08-27View as Markdown

What the badges mean
  • ✅bench Verified on our bench
  • 📖official Official Battle Company docs
  • 🔍app Decoded from the Callsign app
  • 👥community Community-reported
  • 🧪software Built + software-tested
  • 📐spec Specified only

Nothing unconfirmed is published — see Credits & sourcing.

  • ~600 ft— rated maximum reach of the IR beam in daylight (Battle Company). 📖official
  • ~50 %— how much bright direct sunlight shrinks the gun's hit radius. 📖official
  • 980 nm / 38 kHz— the IR wavelength and carrier; a Class 1 device, safe for eyes. 📖official
  • ~18–20 ft— how far a respawn station "hears" a headset-and-trigger request outdoors. ✅bench

Source: docs/reference/brx-manual-notes.md, docs/reference/brx-extended-user-guide.md, docs/reference/grenade.md

Line-of-sight rules of thumb

  • IR is light. It needs a clear path to a sensor dome; it does not go through people, walls or dense foliage. Shade and dusk improve range; full noon sun reduces it. 📖official
  • It bounces. In small rooms and near walls a shot can reflect back onto your own headset. Use indoor mode indoors. ✅bench
  • The headset is the bigger target. Head-height domes on four sides catch far more than the gun's own sensor; snipers aim for the head. 👥community
  • Close range is chaos. Point-blank, every dome reports; at distance the facing dome reports. Neither changes the damage. ✅bench
  • Feedback fades in sun. The headset's green hit LEDs are hard to read in direct sun, so long-range tags look like misses. Listen for the target's hit sound instead. 👥community

Source: docs/reference/brx-manual-notes.md, docs/reference/brx-extended-user-guide.md, docs/reference/community-notes.md, docs/gotchas.md, protocol/brx-protocol.md §7r

Bluetooth range is not IR range. The phone hosting a game must stay within Bluetooth reach of its gun — in practice mounted on it. IR shots still land at hundreds of feet; the game logic is what stops working when the phone is out of range.

Source: protocol/brx-protocol.md §7n
