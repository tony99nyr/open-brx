Quick Start

From the box to your first "TARGET MODE" voice line in about fifteen minutes, plus a charge.

benchofficialcommunityLast verified 2026-08-27How we knowMarkdown

A tagger and a headset side by side, powered, headset dome lit. Short line: "Charge it, pair it, sight it, play."

Source: docs/reference/brx-manual-notes.md

What you are holding. The BRX is a rifle-style infrared tagger (Class 1, 980 nm IR "laser"). The wireless headset carries the hit sensors, and the gun has a sensor too. Out of the box it is a non-scoring system. The gun keeps no score and no clock, so scoring comes from the on-gun game or the phone app.

Source: docs/reference/brx-manual-notes.md, docs/reference/brx-extended-user-guide.md, docs/reference/community-notes.md, protocol/brx-protocol.md §7n

First power-on checklist

  1. Charge the gun first. Plug the two-cell 8.4 V smart charger into the round charging port. Do not use the micro-USB "Programing Port" next to it. The charger LED is red, then green when full. A full charge gives you roughly 8 h of play. The headset takes any USB 5 V supply. official
  2. Power on the gun with the slide switch by the barrel. You should hear a startup sound. A "pop" from the speaker at boot means audio is alive. If it boots silently, you were holding SELECT. That is USB disk mode, so power off and try again. officialcommunity
  3. Power on the headset. Its LEDs cycle through colours ("rainbow") while it is unpaired, then settle. Pairing is automatic. It can take up to 3 minutes when lots of taggers and Bluetooth devices are around. officialbench
  4. Watch the headset settle. A slow rainbow blink that never stops means it is not paired. Go to Pairing the Headset. Once paired it shows the gun's team colour. bench
  5. Set indoor or outdoor mode for where you will play (hold ALT 3 s). It changes IR range and LED brightness, and it sticks across power cycles. official
  6. Sight the laser in target mode before your first real game (hold LEFT while powering on). official
  7. Pick a game on the gun with LEFT/RIGHT, pull the trigger to select, then pull the reload handle to start. official

Source: docs/reference/brx-manual-notes.md, docs/reference/brx-extended-user-guide.md, docs/reference/community-notes.md, docs/experiment-log.md (2026-08-27 headset LED entry)

The controls, in one glance

  • Trigger: fire. In menus it selects, and it cycles weapons or characters. official
  • ALT (orange): alt-fire or perk. Hold 3 s to toggle indoor/outdoor. In the menu it cycles perks. official
  • SELECT: steps through settings. Hold it at power-on for USB disk mode (firmware and sounds). official
  • LEFT / RIGHT (D-pad): cycle modes and teams. Hold LEFT at power-on for target mode. Hold RIGHT at power-on for accessory/headset pairing. officialcommunity
  • Reload handle: reloads in play. Pulling it starts an on-gun game. official
  • Slide power switch: by the barrel. official

Source: docs/reference/brx-manual-notes.md, docs/reference/brx-extended-user-guide.md

Label each gun and its headset as a pair. Put a strip of tape or a sticker with the same mark on both. Stock taggers look identical and come unlabeled. A mixed-up pair is the number one reason for "my gun won't fire" at a party.

Source: docs/reference/community-notes.md, docs/field-process.md
