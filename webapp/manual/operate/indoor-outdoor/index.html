Indoor vs Outdoor Mode

One three-second hold that changes range, LEDs and blast radius.

✅bench📖official👥communityLast verified 2026-08-27View as Markdown

Switching modes

  1. Hold ALT for 3 seconds at any time; the gun announces the new mode. 📖official
  2. The setting persists across power cycles — set it once per venue. 📖official
  3. If you host from the Callsign app, its per-game Outdoor mode toggle sets the same thing. ✅bench

Source: docs/reference/brx-extended-user-guide.md, docs/reference/callsign-ui.md, protocol/brx-protocol.md §7g

What changes

Indoor Outdoor
Green hit LEDs dimmed 📖official full brightness 📖official
RGB headset LEDs enabled 📖official (bright-sun visibility is poor on any headset) 👥community
Explosion / melee range shrunk 📖official full 📖official
Station / respawn-signal reach shorter — the forward IR projection scales with mode ✅bench ~18–20 ft to a respawn station ✅bench
Gun hit radius — bright sunlight cuts it roughly in half (IR noise filtering) 📖official

Source: docs/reference/brx-extended-user-guide.md, docs/reference/community-notes.md, docs/reference/grenade.md

Use indoor mode indoors even if the room is big. Full-power IR in a small space bounces off walls, and a bounce can register on your own headset — we have watched a gun drain its own armor firing at a wall a few feet away.

Source: docs/gotchas.md (Capturing IR), docs/experiment-log.md
