The Grenade & Other Accessories

Pair a thrown grenade, or use it as a respawn point, a hill or a flag. No app needed.

officialbenchcommunityLast verified 2026-08-27How we knowMarkdown

Two different jobs, two different setups. A thrown grenade pairs to your gun, once. An objective device needs no pairing at all, because any gun talks to it over IR. That covers the respawn station, King of the Hill, Assault point and CTF flag. Do not use "install accessory" for objective modes.

Source: docs/reference/grenade.md, docs/reference/brx-extended-user-guide.md

Pairing accessories to your gun (grenade, sidearm, hatchet, shield)

  1. Start with the gun off. Hold RIGHT and power on to reach "install accessory". official
  2. Power on the accessory. It accepts a new pairing for 30 seconds after power-up. official
  3. Aim the gun at it and pull the trigger. The pairing code goes over IR, and the device flashes or chirps to confirm. official
  4. Pair every accessory in the same session, without powering the gun off, then tap SELECT to finish. Adding a new accessory later means re-pairing all of them together. official

Source: docs/reference/brx-extended-user-guide.md

Throwing a paired grenade

  1. Check that your headset is on and paired. official
  2. Press the grenade's top button near your headset to arm it. community
  3. Throw it. On detonation everyone in range takes the blast. Land it on a hill or a respawn point and your team captures that point instantly, at full charge. communitybench Credit: Extreme Laser Tag And More! (grenade videos).

Source: docs/reference/grenade.md

Setting an objective mode on the grenade

  1. Power the grenade off, then on, and wait for the green ready LED. bench
  2. Hold the top button ~4 s until you hear a long loud beep. You are now in setup. bench
  3. Keep holding. It beeps fast and cycles colours. Release on the colour you want. bench
  4. Watch the LED go white to confirm the lock. The mode survives power-cycling, and on every boot it flashes the current mode's colour for about a second. bench
  5. Repeat from step 1 to change the mode. bench

Source: docs/reference/grenade.md

Grenade mode colours

Colour Mode How it plays
Red Frag a plain blast grenade; throw it or trigger it with the button bench
Green Assault shoot it to capture it for your team; the LED turns your colour bench (owners call it finicky community)
Blue Hill (King of the Hill) starts neutral white; shoot to claim it; every shot adds charge and the other team must out-shoot it to take it back; guns announce "control point captured" benchcommunity
Yellow Respawn station starts neutral; shoot to claim it for a team; it then respawns that team's dead players bench
White CTF flag shoot to grab it; a tagger carrying the flag plays the CTF music community

Source: docs/reference/grenade.md, docs/reference/community-notes.md

Using a respawn station

  1. Set the grenade to yellow, put it at your base, and have one player from the team shoot it. It turns that team's colour. bench
  2. Arm each tagger before kickoff. Press the grenade button near each player. Or have each player face the station with the front of the headset and pull the trigger. A tagger that never gets the station signal just self-respawns as normal. bench
  3. Send dead players back to base to respawn. They press the station button, which respawns everyone of that team nearby. Or they face it with the headset front and pull the trigger (about 18–20 ft, less indoors). bench
  4. Expect the other team to overrun it. They shoot it or grenade it to flip its colour. benchcommunity
  5. Missed the arming step? Pressing the station button near a gun during the game forces it into station-respawn mode too. community Credit: Extreme Laser Tag And More! for the mid-game arming path.

Source: docs/reference/grenade.md, docs/field-process.md

A spare tagger left in accessory-setup mode is a free "announcer". It calls out the grenade's mode names as you cycle them. It also says "respawn point enabled" and a claim chime as the game runs. That is handy for setting up a field without staring at LED colours.

Source: docs/reference/grenade.md

Everything you hear from a grenade is really played by the guns and headsets. The explosion, the flashbang, the CTF music and the respawn chime are all their answer to its IR signal. The grenade itself only chirps and flashes. That is why custom sound packs on the gun change "grenade sounds" too.

Source: docs/reference/grenade.md

Grenade quirks owners run into

It can't show a winner.

Stock kit tallies hill and domination scoring nowhere, so a ref or host keeps score. community

The defending team captured their own Assault point.

That is a known quirk. Treat Assault as low-reliability. communitybench

A grenade in the wrong mode triggered odd game reactions

(CTF music in a deathmatch, for example). Check the boot-flash colour before every game. communitybench

The USB-C port is charge-only.

There is no drive, no console and no update path on that port. bench

Source: docs/reference/grenade.md, docs/reference/community-notes.md
