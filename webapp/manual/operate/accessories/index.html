The Grenade & Other Accessories

Pairing a thrown grenade, and using it as a respawn point, hill or flag — no app needed.

📖official✅bench👥communityLast verified 2026-08-27View as Markdown

Two different jobs, two different setups. Thrown grenade = pair it to your gun (once). Objective device (respawn station, King of the Hill, Assault point, CTF flag) = no pairing at all; any gun interacts with it over IR. Do not "install accessory" for objective modes.

Source: docs/reference/grenade.md, docs/reference/brx-extended-user-guide.md

Pairing accessories to your gun (grenade, sidearm, hatchet, shield)

  1. Gun off. Hold RIGHT and power on → "install accessory". 📖official
  2. Power on the accessory. It accepts a new pairing for 30 seconds after power-up. 📖official
  3. Aim the gun at it and pull the trigger — the pairing code goes over IR; the device flashes or chirps to confirm. 📖official
  4. Pair every accessory in the same session without powering the gun off, then tap SELECT to finish. Adding a new accessory later means re-pairing all of them together. 📖official

Source: docs/reference/brx-extended-user-guide.md

Throwing a paired grenade

  1. Your headset must be on and paired. 📖official
  2. Press the grenade's top button near your headset → it arms. 👥community
  3. Throw. On detonation everyone in range takes the blast; landing it on a hill or respawn point instantly captures the point at full charge for your team. 👥community✅bench Credit: Extreme Laser Tag And More! (grenade videos).

Source: docs/reference/grenade.md

Setting an objective mode on the grenade

  1. Power the grenade off, then on; wait for the green ready LED. ✅bench
  2. Hold the top button ~4 s until a long loud beep — you are in setup. ✅bench
  3. Keep holding: it beeps rapidly and cycles colours. Release on the colour you want. ✅bench
  4. The LED goes white to confirm the lock. The mode survives power-cycling; on every boot it flashes the current mode's colour for about a second. ✅bench
  5. To change mode, repeat from step 1. ✅bench

Source: docs/reference/grenade.md

Grenade mode colours

Colour Mode How it plays
Red Frag plain blast grenade — throw or button-trigger ✅bench
Green Assault shoot it to capture for your team; LED turns your colour ✅bench (owners call it finicky 👥community)
Blue Hill (King of the Hill) starts neutral white; shoot to claim; every shot adds charge and the other team must out-shoot it to retake; guns announce "control point captured" ✅bench👥community
Yellow Respawn station starts neutral; shoot to claim for a team; then respawns that team's dead players ✅bench
White CTF flag shoot to grab; a tagger carrying the flag plays the CTF music 👥community

Source: docs/reference/grenade.md, docs/reference/community-notes.md

Using a respawn station

  1. Set the grenade to yellow, place it at base, and have one player from the team shoot it — it turns that team's colour. ✅bench
  2. Arm each tagger before kickoff: press the grenade button near each player, or have each player face the station with the front of the headset and pull the trigger. A tagger that never receives the station signal will just self-respawn as normal. ✅bench
  3. Dead players return to base and respawn either by pressing the station button (everyone of that team nearby) or by facing it with the headset front and pulling the trigger (about 18–20 ft, less indoors). ✅bench
  4. The other team can overrun it — shoot or grenade it to flip its colour. ✅bench👥community
  5. Missed the arming step? Pressing the station button near a gun during the game forces it into station-respawn mode too. 👥community Credit: Extreme Laser Tag And More! for the mid-game arming path.

Source: docs/reference/grenade.md, docs/field-process.md

A spare tagger left in accessory-setup mode is a free "announcer": it calls out the grenade's mode names as you cycle and says "respawn point enabled" and a claim chime as the game runs. Handy for setting up a field without staring at LED colours.

Source: docs/reference/grenade.md

Everything you hear from a grenade — the explosion, the flashbang, the CTF music, the respawn chime — is actually played by the guns and headsets in response to its IR signal. The grenade itself only chirps and flashes. That is why custom sound packs on the gun change "grenade sounds" too.

Source: docs/reference/grenade.md

Grenade quirks owners run into

It can't show a winner.

Hill/domination scoring is not tallied anywhere on stock kit — a ref or host keeps score. 👥community

The defending team captured their own Assault point.

A known quirk; treat Assault as low-reliability. 👥community✅bench

A grenade in the wrong mode triggered odd game reactions

(e.g. CTF music in a deathmatch). Check the boot-flash colour before every game. 👥community✅bench

The USB-C port is charge-only.

There is no drive, no console, no update path on that port. ✅bench

Source: docs/reference/grenade.md, docs/reference/community-notes.md
