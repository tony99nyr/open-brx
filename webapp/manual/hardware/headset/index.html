The headset

A wireless sensor band that decides whether your tagger is allowed to shoot.

✅bench📖official👥communityLast verified 2026-08-27View as Markdown

What the badges mean
  • ✅bench Verified on our bench
  • 📖official Official Battle Company docs
  • 🔍app Decoded from the Callsign app
  • 👥community Community-reported
  • 🧪software Built + software-tested
  • 📐spec Specified only
  • 🚧under construction Under construction

Nothing unconfirmed is published — see Credits & sourcing.

The headset is not an accessory — it is half of the system. It catches most tags, gives visible feedback to the shooter, and the tagger refuses to fire when its headset drops mid-game.

Source: docs/reference/brx-manual-notes.md, docs/reference/community-notes.md

What's on the headset

  • Sensor domes — IR receivers around the band; the wire reports front vs back dome hits separately. ✅bench
  • Green hit LEDs — 3 W, four directions, daylight-visible. 📖official
  • RGB ring — WS2812B addressable LEDs for team colour, rainbow-when-unpaired and feedback. 👥community ✅bench
  • Front IR emitter — the headset can emit too: the melee gesture (v2 "logo" units) and respawn-station requests fire from the front of the headset. 📖official ✅bench
  • Small button — used in the Gen-3 re-pair procedure and in "install accessory" pairing. 👥community
  • PROGRAM pin — a recessed button held at power-on to put the headset into USB disk mode for firmware updates. 📖official

Source: protocol/brx-protocol.md §"$HIR token 1", docs/reference/brx-extended-user-guide.md, docs/reference/community-notes.md, docs/reference/grenade.md

Headset facts

Link to taggerWireless; pairs automatically after power-on. Can take up to 3 minutes with many taggers/BT devices around📖official
Anti-cheat lockoutHeadset drops after game start → tagger locks until it reconnects📖official
Apps need itThe official phone app silently disconnects a tagger that has no linked headset; an externally-hosted game cannot hold a link to a headset-less gun either✅bench
BatterySingle 18650 lithium cell (v2 headsets); charges from any USB 5 V source; v1 has a slide compartment👥community 📖official
FirmwareReported by the tagger as hds.59 on our units✅bench

Before every game: look for rainbow.

A slowly rainbow-cycling headset is unpaired, and its tagger will silently refuse to join a hosted game. Five seconds of eyeballing saves the whole round.

Source: docs/gotchas.md, docs/field-process.md
