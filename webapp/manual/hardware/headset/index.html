The headset

A wireless sensor band that decides whether your tagger is allowed to shoot.

benchofficialcommunityLast verified 2026-09-06How we knowMarkdown

The headset is not an accessory. It is half of the system. It catches most tags and shows the shooter that they hit you. The tagger also refuses to fire when its headset drops mid-game.

Source: docs/reference/brx-manual-notes.md, docs/reference/community-notes.md

What's on the headset

  • Sensor domes: IR receivers around the band. The wire reports front dome hits and back dome hits separately. bench
  • Green hit LEDs: 3 W, pointing four ways, bright enough to see in daylight. official
  • RGB ring: WS2812B addressable LEDs. They show team colour, the rainbow when unpaired, and hit feedback. community bench
  • Front IR emitter: the headset sends IR too. The melee swing (v2 "logo" units) and respawn-station requests fire from the front of the band. official bench
  • Small button: used to re-pair a Gen-3 headset, and in "install accessory" pairing. community
  • PROGRAM pin: a sunken button. Hold it at power-on to put the headset into USB disk mode for firmware updates. official

Source: protocol/brx-protocol.md §"$HIR token 1", docs/reference/brx-extended-user-guide.md, docs/reference/community-notes.md, docs/reference/grenade.md

Before every game, look for rainbow.

A headset cycling slowly through rainbow colours is unpaired. Its tagger will quietly refuse to join a hosted game. Five seconds of looking saves the whole round.

Source: docs/gotchas.md, docs/field-process.md
