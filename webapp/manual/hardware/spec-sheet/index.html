Spec sheet and what's in the box

One page to print. It lists the exact parts, numbers and kit contents for the tagger, the headset and the grenade.

benchofficialcommunityLast verified 2026-09-06How we knowMarkdown

BRX tagger

Form factor

rifle-style, ABS shell, reload handle on the right official

Controls

trigger · reload handle · ALT (orange) · SELECT · LEFT/RIGHT · power slide switch official

Emitter

Class 1 IR laser, 980 nm, 38 kHz, 6.5 µs pulses, 111 nJ/pulse, <18 mm beam at aperture official

Range

up to ~600 ft; best in shade/night official

Sensors

hit sensor on the body + wireless headset official

Indicators

LED bank (mode / team / life gauge) + green kill-confirm in the sight bench official

Audio

on-board speaker; 2,000+ SFX and voice lines; sound pack replaceable over USB bench official

Radio

Bluetooth Classic (Gen1) / BLE Nordic UART (Gen2/3) bench

Ports

charging port · micro-USB programming port bench official

MCU

PJRC Teensy bench

Battery

7.4 V ~2,200 mAh Li-ion (2-cell, reversed polarity) or 6×AA; ~8 h play official community

Manufacturer

Laser Tag Pro / Battle Company, Oak Creek, WI official

BRX headset

Sensors

IR receiver domes around the band (front/back distinguished on the wire) bench

Feedback

3 W green hit LEDs (4 directions) + WS2812B RGB ring official community

Emitter

front IR emitter (melee gesture, respawn requests) official

Link

wireless auto-pair to its tagger; up to 3 min in crowded RF official

Battery

1 × 18650; USB 5 V charging community official

Firmware

USB disk mode via PROGRAM pin official

Smart Grenade

Top button · safety clip · 3 IR emitters + 1 emitter/receiver · RGB status LED · USB-C (charge only) bench
5 modes

Frag · Assault · Hill · Respawn · CTF bench
