# Developer reference
the complete interoperability spec for the BRX tagger + headset: transport, framing, every known command and event with its field map, the `$WEAP` / `$GSET` / `$PSET` / `$SIR` tables, the optical IR word, the USB console, and a copy-paste path from `pip install` to a live game — with the confidence of every fact stated per row.

- [Transport, framing & safety](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/dev/transport/) — How you reach the gun, what a frame looks like, and why nothing here can brick one
- [Command reference](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/dev/commands/) — Every command we know of — host → tagger, tagger → host, headset — with args, meaning and confidence
- [The arm sequence — from `$CLEAR` to a live gun](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/dev/arm-sequence/) — The exact frame order that takes a tagger live, respawns it, and ends the game — captured from the official app and reproduced by our host
- [`$WEAP` — the weapon definition](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/dev/weap/) — Forty-odd comma-separated tokens that make a weapon out of data: damage, cadence, fire mode, ammo, sounds, IR type
- [`$GSET` and `$PSET` — game and player settings](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/dev/gset-pset/) — The two frames that set on-gun rules and the player's pools, identity and voice pack
- [`$SIR` — the incoming-IR effects matrix](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/dev/sir/) — What an IR hit does to this gun is decided by the victim's table, not by the shooter's weapon
- [Events — what the gun tells you](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/dev/events/) — Hits, health, HUD echoes, buttons and telemetry — and the proof that the gun keeps no game state
- [The IR word — what a shot carries through the air](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/dev/ir/) — A 25-bit pulse-width-encoded word on a 38 kHz carrier, decoded from LaserTagMods' base-station source and verified on our own receiver and emitter
- [The USB serial console — `QUERY` and `SETUP`](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/dev/serial-console/) — The micro-USB "Programing Port" is a Teensy serial console with two commands, not the `$` protocol and not SSH
- [Headset, link and what survives](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/dev/headset-link/) — Which state lives where — and what a BLE drop, a headset switch-off, or a power-cycle each wipe
- [Getting started with `brx-mcp`](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/dev/brx-mcp/) — From `pip install` to a live game in six commands — plus the MCP tools for driving a gun from an AI agent
- [Captures — recording and decoding the official app](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/dev/captures/) — How every fact on these pages was obtained, and how to take the next one
