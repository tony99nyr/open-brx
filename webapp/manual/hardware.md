# Meet the BRX
name every part of the tagger, headset and accessories. Explain what each light and port means. Show why the BRX is a simple IR instrument that remembers nothing, which every later section builds on.

- [The BRX at a glance](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/hardware/overview/): A laser tagger shaped like a rifle, a wireless sensor headset, and a smart grenade. Plus what they are not.
- [The tagger, part by part](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/hardware/tagger/): Every button, port, emitter and light on the rifle, and what each one is really for.
- [The IR emitter, the sight, and the sensors](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/hardware/ir-and-sensors/): The invisible beam that carries every tag. Here are the published specs and what we measured.
- [Lights and what they mean](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/hardware/leds/): The gun's LED bank and the headset's ring. Read them like a dashboard.
- [The headset](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/hardware/headset/): A wireless sensor band that decides whether your tagger is allowed to shoot.
- [Battery and power](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/hardware/battery/): A 7.4 V two-cell pack with one nasty surprise: the connector polarity is backwards.
- [Generations: which BRX do you have?](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/hardware/generations/): Gen1 speaks Bluetooth Classic; Gen2 and Gen3 speak BLE. Here is how to tell in 30 seconds.
- [Grenade and accessories](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/hardware/accessories/): What each add-on physically is. How to use them lives in the Accessories & Stations section.
- [Spec sheet and what's in the box](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/hardware/spec-sheet/): One page to print. It lists the exact parts, numbers and kit contents for the tagger, the headset and the grenade.
