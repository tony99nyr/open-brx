# Operating the BRX
get you from "box on the table" to "a game running and understood". Every button hold, every LED and every voice line should mean something to you. Anything that needs a laptop, a cable, or the BLE protocol is pointed at the developer section, not explained here.

- [Quick Start](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/operate/quick-start/): From the box to your first "TARGET MODE" voice line in about fifteen minutes, plus a charge.
- [Charging & Batteries](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/operate/power/): Two ports on the gun, two kinds of charger, one polarity trap.
- [The On-Gun Menu](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/operate/on-gun-menu/): Everything you can set without a phone, and what the gun remembers.
- [Indoor vs Outdoor Mode](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/operate/indoor-outdoor/): One three-second hold changes your range, your LEDs and your blast radius.
- [Sighting the Laser](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/operate/sighting/): Target mode makes the gun harmless, tireless, and honest about where it points.
- [Pairing the Headset](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/operate/headset-pairing/): The number one cause of "my gun won't fire", and the fix for it.
- [The Grenade & Other Accessories](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/operate/accessories/): Pair a thrown grenade, or use it as a respawn point, a hill or a flag. No app needed.
- [The Callsign App](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/operate/callsign-app/): Battle Company's official phone app: what it does, how you build a game, and the three things nobody tells you.
- [Running a Native Game on Stock Kit](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/operate/stock-game/): A walkthrough of a whole match, on-gun or app-hosted, and what every light and voice line means while it runs.
- [Range & Line of Sight](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/operate/range/): What the numbers on the box mean once the sun comes out.
- [Care & Storage](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/operate/care/): Keep it charged, keep it dry, and turn it off between rounds.
