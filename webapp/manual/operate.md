# Operating the BRX
get from "box on the table" to "a game running and understood", and make every button-hold, LED and voice line on the stock kit mean something. Anything that needs a laptop, a cable, or the BLE protocol is pointed at the developer section, not explained here.

- [Quick Start](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/operate/quick-start/) — From the box to the first "TARGET MODE" voice line in about fifteen minutes (plus a charge).
- [Charging & Batteries](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/operate/power/) — Two ports on the gun, two kinds of charger, one polarity trap.
- [The On-Gun Menu](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/operate/on-gun-menu/) — Everything you can set without a phone — and what the gun remembers.
- [Indoor vs Outdoor Mode](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/operate/indoor-outdoor/) — One three-second hold that changes range, LEDs and blast radius.
- [Sighting the Laser](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/operate/sighting/) — Target mode makes the gun harmless, tireless, and honest about where it is pointing.
- [Pairing the Headset](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/operate/headset-pairing/) — The single most common cause of "my gun won't fire" — and the fix for it.
- [The Grenade & Other Accessories](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/operate/accessories/) — Pairing a thrown grenade, and using it as a respawn point, hill or flag — no app needed.
- [The Callsign App](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/operate/callsign-app/) — Battle Company's official phone app: what it actually does, how a game is built, and the three things nobody tells you.
- [Running a Native Game on Stock Kit](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/operate/stock-game/) — A walkthrough of a whole match — on-gun or app-hosted — and what every light and voice line means while it runs.
- [Range & Line of Sight](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/operate/range/) — What the numbers on the box mean once the sun comes out.
- [Care & Storage](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/operate/care/) — Keep it charged, keep it dry, and turn it off between rounds.
