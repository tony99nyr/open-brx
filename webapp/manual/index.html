Manual hub

The Ultimate BRX Manual: everything about the tagger and headset, in one place.

benchofficialcommunityLast verified 2026-08-27How we knowMarkdown

Battle Company's quick manual and the 2018 Extended Guide are partial and scattered. The rest of the knowledge lives in a Facebook group, in LaserTagMods' repos, and in PDFs. We gathered all of it, checked what we could on the bench, and sorted it. Now you can find an answer while standing on the field with a gun in your other hand. officialcommunitybench
