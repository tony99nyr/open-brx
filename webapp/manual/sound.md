- [The sound bank](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/sound/sound-bank/) — All 2166 ids the BRX will play — by family, with durations and the meanings the community has pinned
- [Custom sounds over USB](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/sound/custom-sounds/) — Yes, you can put your own audio on a BRX — no firmware hacking, fully reversible
