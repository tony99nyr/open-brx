# Fix, mod & accessorise
get a broken tagger back into a game in under five minutes with symptom-first ladders; write down the repairs and mods the community really does (with credit); list the accessories and the places owners gather; answer the ten questions every owner asks.

- [Diagnose my tagger](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/fix/diagnose/): Start with what you see. Pick the ladder that matches, then work down it. The headset is the first thing to check.
- [Headset, pairing & Bluetooth](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/fix/pairing/): The headset controls everything: firing, joining a game, and whether a phone can hold a connection.
- [Hits, sound & battery](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/fix/hits-sound-battery/): When the gun fires but nothing lands, nothing is heard, or nothing lasts.
- [Repairs](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/fix/repairs/): What owners fix themselves, what Battle Company sells, and what's a send-it-back.
- [Mods](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/fix/mods/): The community norm is "no permanent modification". Everything rides on the phone bracket or clips to the rail.
- [Accessories](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/fix/accessories/): Grenade, stations, headsets, power and mounts. What each one is, and the gotchas nobody tells you.
- [The community](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/fix/community/): Where BRX owners really solve things, and what each place is good for.
- [FAQ](https://open-brx.iamrossi.workers.dev/manual/fix/faq/): The questions owners ask first.
