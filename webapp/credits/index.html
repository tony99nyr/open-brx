Credits

  • Battle Company: the BRX hardware, the V7 quick manual and the 2018 Extended User Guide.
  • The BRX owner community: repairs, mods, the audio map, field practice.
  • Jay / Extreme Laser Tag And More!: grenade and accessory videos.

How we source this

Facts come from Battle Company's own documents, from the Callsign app, from the owner community, and from testing on our own taggers. Anything unconfirmed, or contradicted between sources, is not published. Official PDFs are linked and never rehosted. The working notes behind this manual are public in the repository.

Policy

Open BRX is independent. It is not endorsed by Battle Company. Stock firmware is never modified. Everything is done over the documented Bluetooth protocol.
