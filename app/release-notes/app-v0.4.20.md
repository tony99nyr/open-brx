## Open BRX phone app 0.4.20

This build installs over 0.4.6 or later. It adds two fixes to 0.4.19.

### Fixed

- **No false "armour up" for a late joiner.** A player who joins a match after it starts no longer hears an "armour
  up" call a few seconds in.
- **The REDEPLOYED card waits for your tagger.** After a quick respawn, the card stays up until your tagger can fire,
  so it never tells you to go before you can shoot.

### Carried from 0.4.19

See `app-v0.4.19.md`.
