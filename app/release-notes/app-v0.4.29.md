## Open BRX phone app 0.4.29

This build installs over 0.4.6 or later. It includes everything in 0.4.28. The Stick firmware floor is still h8-0.3.

### Changed

- **Mission Control holds results more firmly while they are pending** (a Mission Control change). A phone that reached RECEIVED stays received across a drop. Mission Control starts no new match while results are pending. A pending match is kept out of the history until it is released. FINALISE names the phones that are still missing before you confirm.

### Not visible to players

- The match simulator and the balance review gates changed (F602, F603, F609, F610). The weapon and perk numbers did not change.
